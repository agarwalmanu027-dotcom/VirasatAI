Ajanta & Ellora Caves, Maharashtra — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Ajanta and Ellora are distinct sites, not one cave complex.
2. Ajanta contains Buddhist monasteries, worship halls, paintings and sculpture.
3. Ajanta's main excavation phases span the second–first centuries BCE and fifth–sixth centuries CE.
4. Ellora contains thirty-four principal caves belonging to Buddhist, Hindu and Jain traditions.
5. Ellora's Cave 16 is the Kailasa temple.
6. Both sites entered the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1983.
