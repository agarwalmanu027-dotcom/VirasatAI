Bhadra Maruti Temple — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The shrine is in Khuldabad.
2. It is dedicated to Hanuman, also called Maruti.
3. Its principal image presents Hanuman in a reclining pose.
4. A devotional legend links the pose with the hymns of King Bhadrasen.
