Overview
Bibi Ka Maqbara is the tomb of Dilras Banu Begum, wife of Aurangzeb, set within a formal Mughal garden.

Identity and scope notes
Tourism sources give differing precise construction dates; the script uses Mughal-period context without an exact year. This is a separate Deccan mausoleum, not Taj Mahal imagery.
