Bibi Ka Maqbara — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The mausoleum commemorates Dilras Banu Begum, also called Rabia-ud-Durrani.
2. She was the wife of Aurangzeb and mother of Azam Shah.
3. The monument is associated with Azam Shah's patronage.
4. A four-part garden with axial paths and water features frames it.
5. Minarets and marble lattice screens are prominent features.
