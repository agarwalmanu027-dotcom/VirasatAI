Bibi Ka Maqbara — story content

Bibi Ka Maqbara is a Mughal mausoleum in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. It commemorates Dilras Banu Begum, the wife of Aurangzeb, who was also known as Rabia-ud-Durrani. Her son Azam Shah is associated with the monument's patronage.

The garden is essential to its effect. A four-part arrangement, broad paths and axial water features guide the eye toward the tomb. The building is meant to be experienced within that planned landscape.

Minarets frame the central structure, while marble lattice work and floral patterns invite a closer look. Notice the balance between the distant silhouette and the details near a doorway or screen.

Its resemblance to the Taj Mahal has earned a familiar nickname, the Taj of the Deccan. Yet this place has its own setting and commemorative story. Bibi Ka Maqbara deserves attention as a distinct monument of Mughal architecture and remembrance in the Deccan.
