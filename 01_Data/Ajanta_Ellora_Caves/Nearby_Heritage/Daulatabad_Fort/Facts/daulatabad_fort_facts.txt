Daulatabad Fort — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Devgiri, Deogiri and Daulatabad refer to this fort's historical identity.
2. Its origins are associated with the twelfth-century Yadavas.
3. Muhammad bin Tughluq shifted his capital to Daulatabad in the fourteenth century.
4. Defensive features include walls, gates, a moat and a rock-cut passage.
5. Stored water and granaries were part of the fort's infrastructure.
