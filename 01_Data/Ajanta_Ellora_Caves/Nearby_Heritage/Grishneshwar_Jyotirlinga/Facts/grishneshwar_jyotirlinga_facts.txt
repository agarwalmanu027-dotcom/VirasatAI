Grishneshwar Jyotirlinga — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The temple is in Verul near Ellora.
2. It is dedicated to Shiva and revered among the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines.
3. Ahilyabai Holkar is associated with eighteenth-century restoration.
4. The temple includes a carved tiered tower and pillared hall.
5. It remains an active pilgrimage institution.
