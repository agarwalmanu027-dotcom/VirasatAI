Jama Masjid Aurangabad — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The historic city mosque is called Jama or Jumma Masjid.
2. The Gazetteer associates its early seventeenth-century work with Malik Ambar.
3. It records later additions under Aurangzeb.
4. Its documented design includes rows of polygonal pillars, arches, small domical vaults and a courtyard.
5. It is distinct from the city's Shah Ganj mosque and the mosque at Daulatabad.
