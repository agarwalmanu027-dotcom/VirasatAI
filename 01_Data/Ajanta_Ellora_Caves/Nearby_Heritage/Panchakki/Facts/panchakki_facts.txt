Panchakki — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Panchakki means a water mill.
2. It is in the Baba Shah Musafir dargah setting.
3. An underground channel carries water from a source in the hills.
4. Falling water supplied the force used by the mill.
5. The mill is associated with grinding grain for people visiting the religious complex.
