Soneri Mahal — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Soneri Mahal is associated with a Bundelkhand chief in the seventeenth century.
2. The name refers to gold-painted decoration.
3. The palace has two storeys and a raised plinth.
4. It stands in the university-campus setting near the cave foothills.
5. It has been adapted as a museum.
