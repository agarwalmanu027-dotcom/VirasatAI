Soneri Mahal — story content

Soneri Mahal, the Golden Palace, adds a domestic and artistic setting to the heritage of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. Built under a Bundelkhand chief in the seventeenth century, its name recalls paintings worked with gold.

The two-storeyed palace stands on a raised plinth. Its scale, halls and openings create a different experience from a fort's defences or a cave's rock-cut interior. Look at how the building presents itself within its surrounding grounds.

Today, the palace also has a museum role. Regional artefacts and old artistic material connect the house itself with a wider collection of history.

The university-campus setting is another part of its present life. Follow access guidance and respect the exhibits. Soneri Mahal shows how a historic residence can be given a continuing public purpose, preserving the building while helping visitors understand the region through objects and art.
