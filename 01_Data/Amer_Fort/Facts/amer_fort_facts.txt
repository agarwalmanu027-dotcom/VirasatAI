VERIFIED FACTS — Amer Fort, Jaipur
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Amer Fort, also called Amber Palace, forms part of UNESCO's Hill Forts of Rajasthan serial property.
2. Its hilltop setting combines defensive walls with palace courts and gardens.
3. The complex includes audience halls and the mirror-decorated Sheesh Mahal.
4. UNESCO identifies Amber as an important example of the seventeenth-century Rajput-Mughal court style.
