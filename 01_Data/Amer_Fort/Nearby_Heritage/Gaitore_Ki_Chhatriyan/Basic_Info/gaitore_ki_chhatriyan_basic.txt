Overview
Gaitore contains memorial cenotaphs of Jaipur's former rulers.

Identity and scope
Cenotaphs are commemorative structures; do not describe them as ordinary royal burial tombs. Exact column counts and a folk etymology are omitted.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
