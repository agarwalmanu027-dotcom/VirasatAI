VERIFIED FACTS — Gaitore Ki Chhatriyan
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Gaitore contains memorial cenotaphs of Jaipur's former rulers.
2. Chhatri pavilions and carved columns define the memorial architecture.
3. A prominent memorial commemorates Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur's founder.

Cenotaphs are commemorative structures; do not describe them as ordinary royal burial tombs. Exact column counts and a folk etymology are omitted.
