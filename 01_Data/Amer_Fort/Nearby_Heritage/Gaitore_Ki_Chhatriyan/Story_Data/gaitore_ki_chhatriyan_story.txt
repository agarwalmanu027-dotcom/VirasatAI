English master narrative
At Gaitore, royal memory is expressed through a group of cenotaphs. These commemorative pavilions honour former rulers of Jaipur, including the city's founder, Sawai Jai Singh the Second.

The chhatris, or roofed memorial pavilions, create an arrangement of domes, columns and carved surfaces. Rather than treating the complex as one building, compare the individual memorials. Differences in detail help each one retain its own character.

A cenotaph is a structure of remembrance. Understanding that purpose changes the visit from simply looking at attractive stonework to considering the people commemorated here. Gaitore also offers a quieter counterpart to Jaipur's royal palaces. The palace tells us about authority and daily court life; the memorial pavilions ask us to think about how later generations chose to remember their rulers.

ये स्मृति के लिए बनाई गई रचनाएँ हैं। इस उद्देश्य को समझने पर यात्रा केवल सुंदर पत्थर देखने तक सीमित नहीं रहती। गैटोर, जयपुर के शाही महलों का एक शांत साथी भी है। महल अधिकार और दरबारी जीवन की कहानी बताते हैं, जबकि ये छतरियाँ सोचने का अवसर देती हैं कि अगली पीढ़ियों ने अपने शासकों को कैसे याद किया।
