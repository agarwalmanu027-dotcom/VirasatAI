VERIFIED FACTS — Jagat Shiromani Temple
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Jagat Shiromani Temple is in Amer and is associated with Krishna and Vishnu worship.
2. Rajasthan Tourism attributes its construction around 1599–1608 to Queen Kanakwati in memory of Jagat Singh.
3. The temple's architecture and sculptural detail are important to its identity.

The Meera-associated origin of the Krishna image is a religious tradition and is not narrated as a proven historical fact.
