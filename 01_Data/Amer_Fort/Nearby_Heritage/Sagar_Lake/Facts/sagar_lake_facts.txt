VERIFIED FACTS — Sagar Lake
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The selected Sagar Lake is the Sagar Kund behind Amer Fort.
2. Government planning documents associate it with historic water supply for Amer and nearby forts.
3. It is distinct from Maota Lake below Amer Fort and Man Sagar Lake near Jal Mahal.

Do not use pictures of Maota or Man Sagar as substitutes. No current water level or working supply-system claim is made.
