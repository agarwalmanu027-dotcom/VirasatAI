1. Badami, historically Vatapi, was an Early Chalukya capital. [1,2]
2. Four main cave temples were cut into sandstone, with three Hindu caves and one Jain cave. [1,2]
3. Cave one includes Shiva as the eighteen-armed dancing Nataraja; the later caves include Vishnu imagery and Jain Tirthankaras. [1]
4. The broader Aihole–Badami–Pattadakal evolution proposal is on UNESCO's Tentative List; this does not mean Badami caves are an inscribed World Heritage property. [3]
