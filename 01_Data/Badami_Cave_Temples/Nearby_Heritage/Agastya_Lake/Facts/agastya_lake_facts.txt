VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Agastya Lake

1. Agastya Lake is a historic man-made waterbody in Badami's sandstone landscape. [1]
2. The district administration also uses the name Agastya Tirtha for the tank. [2]
3. The lake setting links views of the caves, hills and Bhutanatha temples. [1,2]
4. Religious healing stories are not scientific health claims; the script makes no bathing recommendation.
