VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Aihole (Durga Temple)

1. Aihole's Durga Temple has an apsidal, or rounded-ended, plan. [1,2]
2. Its elevated platform and gallery around the shrine are key architectural features. [1]
3. India's UNESCO Tentative List submission identifies it as an unusual apsidal example among Early Chalukya temples. [2]
4. Aihole's tentative candidature is different from Pattadakal's inscription; the script does not call Aihole an inscribed World Heritage Site.
