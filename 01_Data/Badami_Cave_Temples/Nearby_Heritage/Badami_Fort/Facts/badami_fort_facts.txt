VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Badami Fort

1. Badami Fort occupies a hill above the town, opposite the cave-temple landscape. [1,2]
2. The fort area includes gateways, water-storage features and surviving architectural remains. [1]
3. The fortification history includes later modifications rather than a wholly unchanged Chalukya structure. [1,2]
4. No claim is made about a sixteenth-century Tipu cannon or a single builder of all visible structures.
