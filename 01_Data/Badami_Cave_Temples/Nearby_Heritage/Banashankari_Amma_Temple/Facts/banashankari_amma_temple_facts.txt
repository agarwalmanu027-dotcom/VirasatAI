VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Banashankari Amma Temple

1. Government records identify Banashankari Amma Temple at Cholachagudda near Badami. [1,2]
2. The deity is associated with Shakambhari, a form of Parvati. [1]
3. The Ministry of Tourism's festival record documents a chariot procession and fair associated with the temple. [2]
4. Festival dates and attendance vary; the dated 2023 event is not copied as a future schedule.
