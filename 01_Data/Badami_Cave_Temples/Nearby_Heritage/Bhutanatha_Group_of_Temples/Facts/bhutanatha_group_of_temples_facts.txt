VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Bhutanatha Group of Temples

1. The Bhutanatha temple group stands beside Agastya Lake in Badami. [1,2]
2. The main shrine is associated with Shiva and Early Chalukya building, with later additions. [1]
3. Karnataka Tourism describes an early seventh-century core and an eleventh-century outer hall. [1]
4. These are structural temples, distinct from Badami's rock-cut cave interiors.
