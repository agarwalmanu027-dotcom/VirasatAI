Lad Khan Temple

1. Lad Khan Temple is a named early structural temple at Aihole. [1,2]
2. Karnataka Tourism describes imagery from Shaiva, Vaishnava and Shakti traditions. [1]
3. India's UNESCO Tentative List submission places Ladkhan in the Durga complex. [2]
4. The later name should not be used to infer Islamic original construction; exact early dating and royal-hall origin stories are not asserted here.
