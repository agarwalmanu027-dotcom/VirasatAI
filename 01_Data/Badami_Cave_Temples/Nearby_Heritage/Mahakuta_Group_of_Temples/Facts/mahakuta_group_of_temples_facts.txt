Mahakuta Group of Temples

1. Bagalkote district identifies Mahakuta as an important Hindu temple group associated with Shiva. [1]
2. The principal temples belong to the Early Chalukya period, broadly sixth–seventh centuries. [1]
3. Architectural forms reflect both northern and southern building traditions. [1]
4. Karnataka Tourism describes the complex around a natural spring and tank. [2]
