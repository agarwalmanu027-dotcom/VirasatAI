VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Pattadakal Monuments

1. UNESCO describes Pattadakal as a seventh–eighth-century Chalukya temple group combining northern and southern architectural forms. [1]
2. The property comprises nine Hindu temples and a Jain sanctuary. [1]
3. Queen Lokamahadevi built Virupaksha Temple around 740 to commemorate her husband's victory. [1]
4. The Group of Monuments at Pattadakal was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1987. [1]
