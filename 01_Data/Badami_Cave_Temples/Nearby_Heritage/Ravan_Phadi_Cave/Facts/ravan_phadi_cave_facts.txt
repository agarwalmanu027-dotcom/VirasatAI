VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Ravan Phadi Cave

1. Karnataka Tourism identifies Ravana Phadi as a sixth-century rock-cut cave at Aihole. [1]
2. The cave's sculpture includes Shiva, Parvati, Ganesha and Vishnu imagery. [1]
3. It is distinct from Badami's four principal cave temples. [1,2]
