Ravan Phadi Cave brings rock-cut architecture into the Aihole temple circuit. Also written Ravana Phadi, it is identified by Karnataka Tourism as a sixth-century cave temple. Its sculpture includes Shiva, Parvati, Ganesha and Vishnu, placing religious images within a space cut from rock.

Pause to consider the work behind the room itself. A cave temple required craftsmen to remove material while preserving the shapes intended for pillars and panels. The sculpture and the surrounding architecture therefore belong to one carefully planned act of making.

Look for changes in pose, scale and placement among the figures where viewing is permitted. These choices help an image communicate more than its identity alone. The cave is at Aihole and should not be confused with one of Badami's four main caves. Avoid touching carvings and follow any restrictions on lighting or photography. Ravan Phadi offers a close encounter with the relationship between stone, sacred imagery and architectural imagination.
