VERIFIED FACTS — Charminar, Hyderabad
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Charminar was built by Sultan Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in 1591.
2. It stands at the centre of the historic planned city of Hyderabad.
3. The square structure has four great arches and a minaret at each corner.
4. Granite and lime mortar form part of its construction; an upper level includes a mosque.

Origin stories involving plague, Bhagmati or a secret tunnel are not presented as verified facts. Conflicting monument/minaret height figures are omitted.
