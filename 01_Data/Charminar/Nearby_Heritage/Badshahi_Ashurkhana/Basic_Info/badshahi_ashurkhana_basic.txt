Overview
Badshahi Ashurkhana is a Shia mourning place associated with remembrance of Karbala.

Identity and scope
Report construction-phase dates contain inconsistencies; only broad early Qutb Shahi chronology and documented 1594 commencement are used. It is a mourning hall, not simply another mosque.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
