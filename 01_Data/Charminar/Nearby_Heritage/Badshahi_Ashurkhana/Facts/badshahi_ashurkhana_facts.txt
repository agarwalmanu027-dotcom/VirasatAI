VERIFIED FACTS — Badshahi Ashurkhana
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Badshahi Ashurkhana is a Shia mourning place associated with remembrance of Karbala.
2. Construction began under Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah in 1594.
3. The site is known for surviving early seventeenth-century glazed tilework.
4. Alams, ceremonial standards, form part of its religious significance.

Report construction-phase dates contain inconsistencies; only broad early Qutb Shahi chronology and documented 1594 commencement are used. It is a mourning hall, not simply another mosque.
