Overview
Hayat Bakshi Mosque is in Hayathnagar and is associated with Hayat Bakshi Begum.

Identity and scope
This is the Hayathnagar mosque complex, not the funerary mosque near Hayat Bakshi Begum's tomb in Qutb Shahi Heritage Park. Exact completion date is omitted due to varied chronology.
