VERIFIED FACTS — Hayat Bakshi Mosque
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Hayat Bakshi Mosque is in Hayathnagar and is associated with Hayat Bakshi Begum.
2. Hayat Bakshi Begum was the mother of Abdullah Qutb Shah.
3. The historic complex joins a mosque with sarai accommodation and a large well known as Hathi Bowli.
4. The mosque has a raised terrace, five arched openings and an extensive courtyard.

This is the Hayathnagar mosque complex, not the funerary mosque near Hayat Bakshi Begum's tomb in Qutb Shahi Heritage Park. Exact completion date is omitted due to varied chronology.
