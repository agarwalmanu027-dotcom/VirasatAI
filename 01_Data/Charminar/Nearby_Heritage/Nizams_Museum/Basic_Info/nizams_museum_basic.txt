Overview
The Nizam's Museum is located at Purani Haveli.

Identity and scope
Some secondary accounts use 1936 for jubilee gifts; narration does not use an exact jubilee year. The original Jubilee Pavilion Hall at Public Gardens and present museum location at Purani Haveli are distinguished.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
