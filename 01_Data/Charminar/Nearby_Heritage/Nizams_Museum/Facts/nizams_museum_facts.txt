VERIFIED FACTS — Nizam's Museum
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Nizam's Museum is located at Purani Haveli.
2. Its collection is associated with Mir Osman Ali Khan, the seventh Nizam, and silver jubilee gifts.
3. The official museum identifies 1937 celebrations and subsequent additions to the collection.
4. Displays include models, paintings, ceremonial objects, decorative artefacts and formal addresses.

Some secondary accounts use 1936 for jubilee gifts; narration does not use an exact jubilee year. The original Jubilee Pavilion Hall at Public Gardens and present museum location at Purani Haveli are distinguished.
