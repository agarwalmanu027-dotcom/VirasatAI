Overview
Paigah Tombs is the burial complex of the Paigah noble family of Hyderabad.

Identity and scope
The complex developed across phases. A single construction date or builder is not assigned to every tomb. Unsupported global uniqueness claims are omitted.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
