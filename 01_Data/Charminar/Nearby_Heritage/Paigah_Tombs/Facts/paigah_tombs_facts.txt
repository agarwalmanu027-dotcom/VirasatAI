VERIFIED FACTS — Paigah Tombs
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Paigah Tombs is the burial complex of the Paigah noble family of Hyderabad.
2. The family was closely associated with the Nizams.
3. The complex includes finely carved marble tombstones, lattice screens and lime plaster ornament.
4. Historic tomb enclosures, a mosque and a water body form parts of the ensemble.

The complex developed across phases. A single construction date or builder is not assigned to every tomb. Unsupported global uniqueness claims are omitted.
