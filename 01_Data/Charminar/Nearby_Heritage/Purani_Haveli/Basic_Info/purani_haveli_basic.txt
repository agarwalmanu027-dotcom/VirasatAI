Overview
Purani Haveli is a historic palace associated with the Nizams of Hyderabad.

Identity and scope
Palace and museum are related but distinct catalogue entries. The script introduces the palace setting, while the museum script covers the collection. Jubilee Pavilion at Public Gardens is not treated as the same original building.
