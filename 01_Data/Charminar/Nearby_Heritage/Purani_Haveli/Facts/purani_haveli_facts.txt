VERIFIED FACTS — Purani Haveli
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Purani Haveli is a historic palace associated with the Nizams of Hyderabad.
2. Its documented architecture combines European and Indian influences and large open courtyards.
3. The complex is listed in the metropolitan heritage record.
4. The Nizam's Museum is housed within the Purani Haveli setting.

Palace and museum are related but distinct catalogue entries. The script introduces the palace setting, while the museum script covers the collection. Jubilee Pavilion at Public Gardens is not treated as the same original building.
