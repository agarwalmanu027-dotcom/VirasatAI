Overview
The Qutb Shahi Tombs are the historic necropolis of the dynasty that ruled Golconda in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.

Identity and scope
World Heritage inscription is not claimed. Dynastic tomb counts are not confused with total monuments, and this park is distinct from Paigah Tombs.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
