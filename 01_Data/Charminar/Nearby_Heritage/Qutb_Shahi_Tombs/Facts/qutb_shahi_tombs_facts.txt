VERIFIED FACTS — Qutb Shahi Tombs
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Qutb Shahi Tombs are the historic necropolis of the dynasty that ruled Golconda in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.
2. The wider Heritage Park includes rulers, family members and other court figures.
3. The ensemble includes tombs, funerary mosques, a hammam and stepwells.
4. Many mausoleums have large domes and arched façades; the complex is more extensive than the popular phrase Seven Tombs suggests.

World Heritage inscription is not claimed. Dynastic tomb counts are not confused with total monuments, and this park is distinct from Paigah Tombs.
