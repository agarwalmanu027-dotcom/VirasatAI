VERIFIED FACTS — Toli Masjid
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Toli Masjid is in the historic Karwan locality of Hyderabad.
2. Musa Khan commissioned the mosque in the late Qutb Shahi period, around 1671–72.
3. Its raised plinth, five arched façade bays, large minarets and stucco parapet are documented features.
4. The ornament combines Qutb Shahi mosque and south Indian architectural elements.

The popular damri funding story is not presented as verified fact.
