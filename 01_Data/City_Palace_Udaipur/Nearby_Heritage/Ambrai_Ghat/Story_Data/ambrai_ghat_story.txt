Ambrai Ghat brings you to the edge of Lake Pichola, where Udaipur's buildings can be understood as parts of one waterfront. Also called Manjhi Ghat, this place offers a different experience from entering a palace or museum.

Here, the view itself is the starting point. Notice the relationship between water and architecture. A building seen across a lake has a different scale from the same building seen through its doorway. Reflections and changing light can alter that relationship again.

The ghat is also part of a lived city. Give local people room to use the space, and observe the surroundings without treating everyday life as a performance.

Ambrai helps connect the stops of a heritage visit. Its lakeside setting encourages a wider picture of Udaipur, where public space, water and historic architecture belong to the same continuing urban story.
