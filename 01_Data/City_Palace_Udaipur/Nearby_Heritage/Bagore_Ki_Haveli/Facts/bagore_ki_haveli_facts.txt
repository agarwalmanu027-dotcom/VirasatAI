VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Bagore Ki Haveli

1. The haveli stands beside Lake Pichola at Gangaur Ghat. [1]
2. It was built in the eighteenth century by Amar Chand Badwa, a Mewar prime minister. [1]
3. The West Zone Cultural Centre maintains a museum in the restored haveli. [2]
