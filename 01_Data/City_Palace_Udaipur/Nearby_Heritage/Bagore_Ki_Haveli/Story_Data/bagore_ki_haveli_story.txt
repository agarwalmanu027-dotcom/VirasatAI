Beside Lake Pichola at Gangaur Ghat, Bagore Ki Haveli brings a historic home into the cultural life of modern Udaipur. Built in the eighteenth century by Amar Chand Badwa, a prime minister of Mewar, it is now preserved as a museum and cultural setting.

Its rooms, courtyards and balconies help us imagine domestic architecture on a large scale. Glasswork and paintings reward a close look, while the arrangement of spaces explains how different parts of a residence connect.

The West Zone Cultural Centre's work here links conservation with sharing culture. A restored building can hold objects, performances and learning, allowing heritage to remain part of public life.

Look beyond the lakeside facade. Bagore's story is also about the people who built, used and cared for its spaces. In this haveli, architectural memory and Rajasthan's continuing artistic traditions meet within the same historic walls.
