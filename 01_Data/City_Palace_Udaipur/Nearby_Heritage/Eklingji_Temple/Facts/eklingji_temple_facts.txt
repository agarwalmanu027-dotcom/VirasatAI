Eklingji Temple

1. Eklingji is a Shiva temple associated with Mewar's rulers. [1,2]
2. Its deity is represented by a four-faced black stone image. [1]
3. Mewar's institutional account identifies Shri Eklingji as the dynasty's personal deity. [2]
