Shilpgram

1. Shilpgram is a rural arts and crafts complex established by the West Zone Cultural Centre. [1]
2. The complex presents regional rural and tribal cultures through built spaces and cultural activities. [1,2]
3. It is a modern cultural institution, not an ancient excavated village. [1]
