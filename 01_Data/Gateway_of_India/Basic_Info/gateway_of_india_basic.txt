Overview
The Gateway of India stands at Apollo Bunder overlooking the Arabian Sea.

Identity and scope
Foundation dates vary in online descriptions and are omitted. This is Mumbai's Gateway of India, not Delhi's India Gate. Current ferry arrangements are not asserted.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
