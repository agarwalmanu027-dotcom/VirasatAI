VERIFIED FACTS — Gateway of India, Mumbai
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Gateway of India stands at Apollo Bunder overlooking the Arabian Sea.
2. It commemorates the 1911 visit of King George V and Queen Mary and was completed in 1924.
3. George Wittet designed the Indo-Saracenic monument, which rises about 26 metres.
4. The arch combines a large central opening, dome and Indian and Islamic architectural motifs.

Foundation dates vary in online descriptions and are omitted. This is Mumbai's Gateway of India, not Delhi's India Gate. Current ferry arrangements are not asserted.
