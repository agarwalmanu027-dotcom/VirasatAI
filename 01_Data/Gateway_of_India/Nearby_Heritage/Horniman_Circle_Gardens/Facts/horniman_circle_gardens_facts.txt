VERIFIED FACTS — Horniman Circle Gardens
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Horniman Circle Gardens occupies the former Elphinstone Circle in South Mumbai.
2. The earlier Bombay Green was reorganised as a garden surrounded by buildings.
3. The garden was renamed after independence in honour of Benjamin Horniman, editor of the Bombay Chronicle and supporter of Indian independence.
4. Historic photographs record the garden and its surrounding urban form.

Smithsonian finding aid prints a contradictory completion year; exact garden completion year is omitted.
