Overview
Khotachiwadi is a chiefly residential heritage precinct in Mumbai.

Identity and scope
This is a living residential precinct, not a reconstructed museum village. Private interiors and resident participation are not offered.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
