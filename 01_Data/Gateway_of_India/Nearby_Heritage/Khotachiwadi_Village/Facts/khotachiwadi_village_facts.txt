VERIFIED FACTS — Khotachiwadi Village
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Khotachiwadi is a chiefly residential heritage precinct in Mumbai.
2. Its architectural character includes narrow street edges, pitched tile roofs, porches and verandahs.
3. Timber details and external staircases are characteristic features of documented houses.
4. Heritage studies emphasise both architectural and community value.

This is a living residential precinct, not a reconstructed museum village. Private interiors and resident participation are not offered.
