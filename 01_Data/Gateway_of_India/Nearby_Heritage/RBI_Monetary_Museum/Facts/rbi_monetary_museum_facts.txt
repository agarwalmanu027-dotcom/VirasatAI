VERIFIED FACTS — RBI Monetary Museum
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. RBI's Monetary Museum is located in Amar Building in Fort, Mumbai.
2. President A. P. J. Abdul Kalam inaugurated it on 18 November 2004.
3. Its displays explain monetary evolution through coins, banknotes and financial instruments.
4. The museum's purpose includes documenting and presenting India's monetary heritage.
