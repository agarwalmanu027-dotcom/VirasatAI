Overview
Sewri Fort stands on a hillock on Mumbai's eastern waterfront.

Identity and scope
The Heritage Project page contains contradictory Portuguese/British attribution; narration follows the MMR-HCS inventory. Proposed museum and improvements are future plans, not completed visitor facilities.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
