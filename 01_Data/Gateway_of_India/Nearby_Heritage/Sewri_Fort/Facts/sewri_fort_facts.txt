VERIFIED FACTS — Sewri Fort
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Sewri Fort stands on a hillock on Mumbai's eastern waterfront.
2. MMR-HCS's inventory dates the British fort to 1680.
3. Its basalt construction includes an arched entrance, walls, bastions and a vaulted corridor.
4. The fort's historic role was defensive observation of the eastern sea front.

The Heritage Project page contains contradictory Portuguese/British attribution; narration follows the MMR-HCS inventory. Proposed museum and improvements are future plans, not completed visitor facilities.
