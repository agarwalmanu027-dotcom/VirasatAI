Golden Temple, Amritsar — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Guru Arjan developed the central shrine and installed the scripture there in 1604.
2. The shrine stands in the sarovar and is approached by a causeway.
3. Four entrances express welcome across social and religious divisions in the institution's interpretation.
4. Maharaja Ranjit Singh contributed to its later embellishment.
5. The complex is managed by the Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee.
