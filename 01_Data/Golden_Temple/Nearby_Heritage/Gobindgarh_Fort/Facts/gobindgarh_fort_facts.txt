Gobindgarh Fort — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The fort's origins are associated with Gujar Singh Bhangi in the eighteenth century.
2. Maharaja Ranjit Singh took it in 1809.
3. It was renamed for Guru Gobind Singh.
4. The fort has a square defensive plan with bastions.
5. Its later history includes British and Indian military use.
