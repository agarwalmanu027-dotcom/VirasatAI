Gobindgarh Fort — story content

Gobindgarh Fort tells a story of changing power through the same defensive walls. Its beginnings are associated with Gujar Singh Bhangi in the eighteenth century. Maharaja Ranjit Singh took the fort in eighteen hundred and nine and named it in honour of Guru Gobind Singh.

The square plan and projecting bastions help explain its military purpose. A fort had to control approaches and protect the people and resources held inside. Look at its overall shape before focusing on individual buildings.

The site later passed through British and Indian military use. Its conversion into a public heritage attraction gives another layer to that long history.

As you move through permitted spaces, distinguish the old defensive fabric from modern displays. Both contribute to a visit, but they tell different parts of the story. Gobindgarh reveals how a place built for security can become a place for learning about Amritsar's past.
