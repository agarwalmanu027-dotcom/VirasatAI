Gurdwara Mata Kaulan — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The gurdwara is also called Kaulsar Sahib.
2. It commemorates Bibi Kaulan, a devotee associated with Guru Hargobind.
3. Kaulsar is the sacred pool connected with the place.
4. The pool's excavation is described as taking place between 1624 and 1627.
