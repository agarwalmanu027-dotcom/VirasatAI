Harike Wetland — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Harike lies at the confluence of the Beas and Sutlej.
2. It includes a shallow reservoir created by river damming.
3. It was designated a Ramsar site in 1990.
4. The wetland supports waterbirds, aquatic life and local livelihoods.
5. Siltation, invasive water plants and pollution are documented pressures.
