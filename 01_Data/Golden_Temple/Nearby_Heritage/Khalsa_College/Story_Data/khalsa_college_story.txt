Khalsa College — story content

Khalsa College adds the history of education to Amritsar's heritage. Founded in eighteen ninety-two, the college grew from a movement to provide learning and opportunity, with leaders of the Singh Sabha involved in its beginnings.

Its campus building is associated with the architect Ram Singh. The scale and carefully arranged architectural features give an educational institution the presence of a major city landmark.

Yet the most important story is not limited to the exterior. Classrooms, libraries, laboratories and student life connect the founding ambition with later generations. The place was created for learning, and that purpose continues.

Observe the architecture from permitted areas and respect academic activity. Khalsa College helps us understand that heritage includes institutions which shaped people's futures. Its lasting value comes from the meeting of a memorable building with the everyday work of teaching and study.
