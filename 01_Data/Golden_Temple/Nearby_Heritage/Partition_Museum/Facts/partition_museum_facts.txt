Partition Museum — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Amritsar museum is housed in the historic Town Hall.
2. Its collections include refugee artefacts, art, archival material and oral histories.
3. Its subject is the Partition of the Indian subcontinent in 1947 and its human consequences.
4. The institution also has a separate Delhi museum.
