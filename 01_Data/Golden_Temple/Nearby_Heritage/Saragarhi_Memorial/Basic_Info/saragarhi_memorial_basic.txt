Overview
Amritsar's Saragarhi memorial remembers the twenty-one Sikh soldiers who died defending Saragarhi in 1897.

Identity and scope notes
Identity resolved as Gurdwara Saragarhi in Amritsar. This is a commemorative site, not the original battlefield or Ferozepur memorial. Exaggerated opponent numbers are excluded.
