Saragarhi Memorial — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Amritsar heritage walk lists Gurdwara Saragarhi.
2. The remembered battle took place on 12 September 1897.
3. Twenty-one men of the 36th Sikhs defended the Saragarhi post.
4. The battlefield was in the north-west frontier region, now in Pakistan.
5. Amritsar's memorial is distinct from the Saragarhi memorial in Ferozepur.
