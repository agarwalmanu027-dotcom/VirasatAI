Badavilinga Temple — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The shrine contains a monolithic Shiva linga.
2. Official tourism describes it as about three metres high.
3. The linga stands within a stone chamber near Lakshmi Narasimha.
4. A water-associated base is a distinctive feature.
