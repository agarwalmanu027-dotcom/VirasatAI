Matanga Hill — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Matanga Hill lies east of the Virupaksha temple street.
2. It overlooks Hampi and the Tungabhadra landscape.
3. A Veerabhadra temple forms part of the hill's religious setting.
4. The connection with Sage Matanga is a local legend.
