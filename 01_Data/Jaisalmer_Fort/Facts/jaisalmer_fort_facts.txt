VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Jaisalmer Fort, Rajasthan

1. Jaisalmer Fort is associated with Rawal Jaisal and the city's twelfth-century foundation. [1]
2. Its yellow sandstone gives it the name Sonar Qila, or Golden Fort. [1]
3. The fort contains an inhabited urban area with homes, temples and commercial activity. [1,2]
4. It is one of six components of UNESCO's Hill Forts of Rajasthan property, inscribed in 2013. [2]
