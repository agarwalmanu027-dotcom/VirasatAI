VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Akal Wood Fossil Park

1. Akal Wood Fossil Park preserves fossilised wood in the Jaisalmer region. [1]
2. Official tourism interpretation places the remains roughly 180 million years in the past. [1]
3. Fossils indicate an environment very different from the region's present desert appearance. [1,2]
4. No visitor should collect, rub, carve or climb fossil material.
