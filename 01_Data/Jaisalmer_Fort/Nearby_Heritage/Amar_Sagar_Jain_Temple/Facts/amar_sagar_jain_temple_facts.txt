Amar Sagar Jain Temple

1. The Rajasthan Foundation district profile identifies a carved Jain temple at one end of the Amar Sagar lake setting. [1]
2. Amar Sagar is associated with a lake and the neighbouring Amar Singh Palace. [1,2]
3. A founding date or dedication to a named Tirthankara is not asserted without a stronger institutional temple record.
