VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Bada Bagh

1. Bada Bagh contains chhatris commemorating Jaisalmer's rulers. [1,2]
2. The sandstone pavilions are cenotaphs or memorials; narration does not claim that bodies are buried beneath them. [1]
3. Their columns, canopies and carving form a distinctive memorial landscape. [1]
