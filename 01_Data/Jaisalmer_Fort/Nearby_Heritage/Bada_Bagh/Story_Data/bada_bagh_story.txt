At Bada Bagh, sandstone pavilions turn remembrance into architecture. These chhatris commemorate members of Jaisalmer's ruling family. A chhatri is a memorial canopy, and here its columns and roof create a form that is both open to the landscape and marked as a place of memory.

Look first at the group as a whole, then compare individual structures. Differences in size, proportion and ornament show that a repeated architectural idea can still produce variety. The changing light makes the carved surfaces easier or harder to read, drawing attention to a detail you might have missed a moment earlier.

A cenotaph honours a person; it should not automatically be described as a burial containing their remains. That distinction helps us understand the memorial purpose of this site. Move carefully around the structures and avoid touching delicate carving. Bada Bagh invites a quieter kind of visit, focused on craftsmanship, remembrance and the ways a community gives lasting shape to loss.
