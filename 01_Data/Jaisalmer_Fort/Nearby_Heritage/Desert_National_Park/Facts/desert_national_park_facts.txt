VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Desert National Park

1. The Rajasthan State Biodiversity Board identifies Desert National Park in the Jaisalmer–Barmer region. [1]
2. The area supports desert wildlife including chinkara, foxes and the Great Indian Bustard. [1,2]
3. Its habitat includes rocky, sandy and other arid landforms. [2]
4. Wildlife sightings and current permissions are not guaranteed.
