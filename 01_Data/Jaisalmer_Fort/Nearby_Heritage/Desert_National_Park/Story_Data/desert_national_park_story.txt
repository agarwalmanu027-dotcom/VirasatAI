Desert National Park changes the idea that a desert is empty. Across this protected landscape in the Jaisalmer and Barmer region, sandy and rocky ground supports plants, animals and birds adapted to arid conditions. The Great Indian Bustard is one of its most significant birds, alongside wildlife such as chinkara and desert foxes.

Look carefully before deciding that there is nothing to see. A shape against the ground, a moving shadow or a small change in colour may draw attention to an animal that blends into its surroundings. The habitat matters even when wildlife remains out of sight.

This is a place to explore only through permitted arrangements. Follow authorised routes and guidance, keep a respectful distance and never chase an animal for a photograph. A sighting cannot be promised, and that uncertainty is part of observing nature responsibly. The park adds a living ecological dimension to Rajasthan's heritage, showing why an arid landscape deserves care as well as admiration.
