VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Kuldhara Village

1. Rajasthan Tourism identifies Kuldhara as an abandoned Paliwal Brahmin village. [1]
2. The village is described as protected by the State Archaeology Department. [1]
3. Stories of a curse and an overnight departure are presented as folklore; they do not establish the cause of abandonment. [1,2]
