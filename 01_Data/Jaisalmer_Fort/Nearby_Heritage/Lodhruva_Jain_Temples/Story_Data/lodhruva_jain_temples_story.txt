The Jain temples of Lodhruva remind us that the history of Jaisalmer extends beyond its present fort and streets. Official tourism accounts identify Lodhruva, also written Lodurva, as a former capital and a place known for Jain worship.

At a sacred site, architecture can be appreciated at several scales. Step back to see the overall shape of a temple, then look more closely at the rhythm of columns, openings and carved surfaces where access is allowed. Repeated forms create order, while small differences reveal the attention of individual craftsmen.

The buildings are also places of devotion. Follow the temple's instructions about shoes, photography and entry, and give worshippers the space they need. Avoid assuming that every surviving part belongs to one construction campaign unless a reliable site record confirms it. Lodhruva offers a useful pause in a desert journey: a chance to connect a former centre of settlement with a religious tradition that remains meaningful to its followers.
