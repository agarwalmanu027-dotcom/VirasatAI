VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Nathmal Ji Ki Haveli

1. Official tourism material identifies Nathmal ki Haveli as a nineteenth-century residence associated with Diwan Nathmal. [1]
2. Its elaborate sandstone facade includes carved decorative detail and elephant forms. [1]
3. The familiar account of two architect brothers working from opposite sides is a traditional construction story, not necessary to treat as independently established evidence. [1]
4. Access to private residential areas is not promised.
