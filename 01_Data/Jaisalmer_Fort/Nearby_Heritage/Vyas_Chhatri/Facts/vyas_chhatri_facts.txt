VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Vyas Chhatri

1. The official tourism destination is named Vyas Chhatri and features sandstone memorial pavilions. [1]
2. The site is traditionally associated with the sage Ved Vyasa and Brahmin remembrance. [2]
3. It is treated separately from the royal cenotaphs at Bada Bagh; no unverified precise location or founding date is supplied.
