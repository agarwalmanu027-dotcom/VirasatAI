Khajuraho Monuments, Madhya Pradesh — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The principal temple-building period was under the Chandelas in the tenth and eleventh centuries.
2. The monuments include Hindu and Jain temples.
3. They are grouped into western, eastern and southern clusters.
4. Sandstone temples stand on raised platforms with towers above their sanctums.
5. The group entered the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1986.
