Ajaigarh Fort — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Ajaigarh and Ajaygarh name the same fort in this entry.
2. It is associated with Chandela power in Bundelkhand.
3. Its hilltop position served defence and administration.
4. Ruins include gates, palatial spaces and temples.
5. Rock-cut tanks named Ganga and Yamuna are near its northern gate.
