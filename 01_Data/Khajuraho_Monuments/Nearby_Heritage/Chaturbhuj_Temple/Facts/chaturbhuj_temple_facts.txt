Chaturbhuj Temple — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The temple is in Khajuraho's southern group.
2. It is dedicated to Vishnu.
3. Its central Vishnu sculpture is a major feature.
4. The entrance has carved river-goddess figures Ganga and Yamuna.
