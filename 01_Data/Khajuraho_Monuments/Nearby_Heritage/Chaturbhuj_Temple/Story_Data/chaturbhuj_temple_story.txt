Chaturbhuj Temple — story content

Chaturbhuj Temple offers a quieter way to read Khajuraho's art. Located in the southern group and dedicated to Vishnu, it gives special prominence to the large sacred image within.

At the entrance, carved figures of the river goddesses Ganga and Yamuna mark the threshold. Their position helps show that an entrance was a meaningful part of the journey toward the sanctum.

The main Vishnu figure draws attention through its scale and presence. Spend time with the whole image before looking for individual details. A carefully composed figure can organise the experience of an entire interior.

Compare this temple with the more crowded carved surfaces elsewhere in Khajuraho. Chaturbhuj reminds us that artistic effect depends on placement, proportion and focus as well as quantity. Respect access rules and give the image and surrounding stonework the care that old sculpture needs.
