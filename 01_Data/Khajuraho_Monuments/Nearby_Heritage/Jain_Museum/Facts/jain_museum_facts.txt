Jain Museum — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Jain Museum is also known as Sahu Shanti Prasad Jain Kala Sangrahalaya.
2. Its campus displays sculptures of the twenty-four Jain Tirthankaras.
3. Its exhibits explain Jain religious and cultural traditions.
4. It is distinct from the Jain gallery in the ASI Archaeological Museum.
