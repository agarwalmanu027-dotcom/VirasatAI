Matangeshwar Temple — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The temple is dedicated to Shiva.
2. Its early form belongs broadly to the tenth-century Chandela period.
3. The building is comparatively plain beside many Khajuraho temples.
4. A large linga stands inside.
5. It remains an active place of worship.
