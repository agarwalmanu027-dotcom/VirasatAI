Overview
Konark presents the chariot of Surya in stone, combining a monumental temple plan with richly carved wheels and sculptural panels.

Identity and scope notes
UNESCO's detailed statement supports the thirteenth-century date; an older Odisha Tourism page has an inconsistent twelfth-century date and is not used for dating. Horse counts differ between UNESCO's short and detailed descriptions, so the narration does not give a number. Magnet, floating-idol and dramatic builder stories are omitted. No distances or current access arrangements are assumed.
