Konark Sun Temple, Odisha — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. The temple was built in the thirteenth century under Narasimha Deva I of the Eastern Ganga dynasty.
2. The design represents the chariot of Surya, the Sun God, with twenty-four carved wheels.
3. The surviving complex includes the prominent Jagamohana and the roofless dance hall.
4. Sculptural scenes include musicians, dancers, animals and aspects of contemporary life.
5. The site was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1984.
6. The Archaeological Survey of India undertakes conservation work at the monument.
