Barahi Temple (Chaurasi) — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Barahi and Varahi are alternate spellings used for this shrine at Chaurasi.
2. The temple is in the Prachi valley of Odisha.
3. It is dedicated to the goddess Varahi.
4. The Odisha government annual reference describes its roof as barrel-vaulted.
5. This form belongs to the Khakhara order of Odisha temple architecture.
