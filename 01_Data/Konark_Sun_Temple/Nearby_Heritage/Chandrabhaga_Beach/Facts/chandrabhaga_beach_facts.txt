Chandrabhaga Beach — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Chandrabhaga Beach is in the Konark region of Puri district.
2. It faces the Bay of Bengal and is associated with sunrise views.
3. Magha Saptami is an important festival tradition associated with the beach.
4. Odisha Tourism documents the International Sand Art Festival at Chandrabhaga.
