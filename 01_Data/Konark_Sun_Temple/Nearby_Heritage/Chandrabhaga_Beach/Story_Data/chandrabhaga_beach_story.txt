Chandrabhaga Beach — story content

Chandrabhaga Beach opens the Konark landscape towards the Bay of Bengal. Its sandy shore and wide horizon offer a different experience from the carved stone and enclosed spaces of the Sun Temple.

The beach is associated with sunrise views and the Magha Saptami festival tradition. It also provides a setting for the International Sand Art Festival, where artists shape temporary works directly from the sand.

These connections make the shore a place of nature, devotion and creativity. Stone carving preserves an image for centuries; sand art can change or disappear with weather and time. Both invite us to notice the skill behind a carefully shaped form.

Enjoy the coastline from safe areas and follow local guidance around the sea. Take litter away and give festival activities space. Chandrabhaga expands a heritage journey beyond buildings, showing how a landscape can carry shared traditions while continuing to change with every tide.
