Chausathi Yogini Temple — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. This entry identifies the Chausathi Yogini Temple at Hirapur near Bhubaneswar.
2. It is distinct from the Yogini temple at Ranipur Jharial in western Odisha.
3. Official tourism literature places Hirapur's shrine in the ninth century.
4. The temple has a circular, open-roofed form.
5. Its sculpted Yogini figures occupy niches around the enclosure.
