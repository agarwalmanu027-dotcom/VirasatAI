Dhauli Shanti Stupa — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Dhauli Shanti Stupa is also called the Peace Pagoda.
2. It was built in 1972 by the Japan Buddha Sangha and the Kalinga Nippon Buddha Sangha.
3. Dhauli hill stands in a landscape associated with the Kalinga War and Ashoka.
4. The peace pagoda is a modern monument, distinct from the ancient Ashokan rock edicts in the Dhauli area.
5. The Daya River forms part of the surrounding landscape.
