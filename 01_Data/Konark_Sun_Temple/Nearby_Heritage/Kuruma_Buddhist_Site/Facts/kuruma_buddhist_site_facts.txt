Kuruma Buddhist Site — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Kuruma is a village archaeological site in the Konark region of Puri district.
2. The district administration identifies the remains as a Buddha Vihar, or Buddhist monastery.
3. Recorded discoveries include a Buddha image in the earth-touching pose and an image of Heruka.
4. The site includes remains built with ancient bricks.
