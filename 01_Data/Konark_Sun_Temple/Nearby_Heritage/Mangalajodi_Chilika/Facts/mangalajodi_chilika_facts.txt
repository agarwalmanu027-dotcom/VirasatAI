Mangalajodi (Chilika) — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Mangalajodi is a village and wetland destination in the Chilika region.
2. The wetland supports resident birds and seasonal migratory birds.
3. Odisha Tourism describes a transition from bird poaching to community-led bird protection.
4. Traditional boats are used for observing the channels and wetland landscape.
5. The selected entry is Mangalajodi, not every part of Chilika Lake.
