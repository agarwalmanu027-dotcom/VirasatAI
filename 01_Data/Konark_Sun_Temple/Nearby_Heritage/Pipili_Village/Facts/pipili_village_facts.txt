Pipili Village — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Pipili is a craft settlement in Puri district.
2. Its well-known textile craft is appliqué, locally called Chandua.
3. The work combines pieces of coloured fabric through stitching.
4. Products include umbrellas, canopies, wall hangings and bags.
5. Pipili appliqué is connected with the decoration of Jagannath's festival chariots.
