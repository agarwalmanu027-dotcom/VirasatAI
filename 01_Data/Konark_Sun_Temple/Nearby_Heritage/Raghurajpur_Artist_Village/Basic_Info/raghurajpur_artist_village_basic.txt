Overview
Raghurajpur is known for Pattachitra painting and other traditional arts practised in an artisan-village setting.

Identity and scope notes
Variable household counts, claims that every present resident is an artisan and an exact age for the whole tradition are omitted. Paintings are living craft objects; ask before using artists' work commercially or photographing workshop activity.
