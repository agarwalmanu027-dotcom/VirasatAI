Raghurajpur Artist Village — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-08

1. Raghurajpur is a heritage craft village in the Puri region.
2. It is particularly associated with Pattachitra painting on prepared cloth.
3. Pattachitra includes mythological scenes and has links with Jagannath traditions.
4. The village is also associated with palm-leaf engraving and other craft practices.
5. Odissi dancer Kelucharan Mohapatra was born in Raghurajpur.
