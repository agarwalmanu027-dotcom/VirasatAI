Overview
Ramachandi Temple is a sacred place dedicated to Maa Ramachandi in the landscape where the Kushabhadra River meets the Bay of Bengal.

Identity and scope notes
This entry is the Ramachandi shrine near Konark, not other same-named temples elsewhere in Odisha. The temple and beach are related places but are not interchangeable image subjects. No unverified construction date or divine historical event is asserted.
