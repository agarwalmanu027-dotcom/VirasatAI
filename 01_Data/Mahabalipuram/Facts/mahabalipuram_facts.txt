VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Mahabalipuram, Tamil Nadu

1. Mahabalipuram and Mamallapuram refer to the same coastal town. The UNESCO property is the Group of Monuments at Mahabalipuram. [1]
2. Pallava patrons created the principal monuments in the seventh and eighth centuries. [1]
3. The group includes cave sanctuaries, monolithic rathas, sculpted reliefs and structural temples. [1]
4. The property was inscribed on the World Heritage List in 1984. [1]
