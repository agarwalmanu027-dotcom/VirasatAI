VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Alamparai Fort

1. Tamil Nadu Archaeology identifies Alambarai/Alamparai Fort near Kadapakkam on the Bay of Bengal. [1]
2. The department places the brick fort's development in the late seventeenth-century Mughal-period context. [1]
3. Its history includes Carnatic rulers, a transfer to French authority in 1750 and British destruction in 1760. [1]
4. Excavations recorded pottery, metal objects and other evidence of activity and exchange. [2]
