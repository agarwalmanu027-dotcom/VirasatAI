Cholamandal Artists' Village

1. Cholamandal Artists' Village was established at Injambakkam in 1966. [1,2]
2. Its community developed homes, studios and shared artistic facilities through collective effort. [1]
3. The village is associated with the Madras Movement of Art and modern South Indian art. [1]
4. The government museum directory records paintings, graphics, drawings, sculpture and artists' studios. [2]
