VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
DakshinaChitra

1. DakshinaChitra is a Madras Craft Foundation heritage museum at Muttukadu. [1,2]
2. The museum opened to the public in December 1996. [1]
3. Eighteen heritage houses were dismantled and reconstructed to preserve regional building traditions. [1]
4. Its work includes crafts, folk performance, exhibitions and education. [1]
