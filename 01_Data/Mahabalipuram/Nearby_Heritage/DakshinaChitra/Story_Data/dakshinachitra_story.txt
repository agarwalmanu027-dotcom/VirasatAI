DakshinaChitra makes everyday life the centre of a heritage visit. At Muttukadu, this museum of the Madras Craft Foundation brings together houses, objects, crafts and cultural practices from South India. It opened to the public in December nineteen ninety-six.

One of its distinctive features is a collection of eighteen heritage houses. Buildings that might otherwise have been lost were dismantled and reconstructed here, allowing visitors to study regional ways of building. A doorway, an interior space or a roof can tell you about materials, climate and the habits of the people who used a home.

The museum also works with crafts, folk performance and education. These activities remind us that culture includes skills and knowledge, not only objects behind glass. Look closely at a household detail and consider the work involved in making it. Follow the day's programme rather than assuming every demonstration is always available. DakshinaChitra offers a thoughtful way to connect architecture with the people and practices that gave it life.
