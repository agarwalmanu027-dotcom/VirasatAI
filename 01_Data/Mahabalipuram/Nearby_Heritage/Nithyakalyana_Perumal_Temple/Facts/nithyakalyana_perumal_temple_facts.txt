VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Nithyakalyana Perumal Temple

1. HRCE identifies Arulmigu Nithya Kalyana Perumal Temple at Thiruvidanthai, Chengalpattu, as TM001839. [1]
2. The temple's deity is associated with Lakshmi Varaha Perumal and its goddess with Komalavalli Thayar. [1]
3. It is recognised in the temple record as one of the Vaishnava Divya Desams. [1]
4. Sri Thiruvidandhai is a spelling/label overlap with this temple's locality, not evidence of a second monument. [1,2]
