VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Poonjeri Village

1. Tamil Nadu Rural Development G.O.50 of 28 March 2008 identifies Poonjeri near Mamallapuram as the site of a sanctioned Sculptors' Park project. [1]
2. The project formed part of livelihood support under the tsunami recovery programme and involved a sculptors' welfare association. [1]
3. The 2008–09 policy note describes planned infrastructure for tsunami-affected master sculptors and artisans. [2]
4. These historical records do not establish present visitor access or an unbroken lineage from Pallava craftsmen.
