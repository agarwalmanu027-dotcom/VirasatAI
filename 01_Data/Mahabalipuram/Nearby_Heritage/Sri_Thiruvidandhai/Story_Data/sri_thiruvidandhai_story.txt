Sri Thiruvidandhai, also written as Thiruvidanthai, is the village setting of Nithyakalyana Perumal Temple in Tamil Nadu. The shrine is associated with Lakshmi Varaha Perumal and remains a place of living Vaishnava worship.

As you explore the setting, consider the connection between a temple and the community around it. A sacred place is part of everyday life: people come to pray, follow their traditions and share a space that carries meaning across generations. This relationship gives the village a cultural character as well as a religious one.
