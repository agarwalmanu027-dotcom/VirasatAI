1. Tiger Cave is a rock-cut site at Saluvankuppam. [1,2]
2. Its name refers to the animal heads framing the carved pavilion; the forms are commonly interpreted as mythical yalis. [2]
3. ASI identifies a tiger-headed rock-cut temple in its protected inventory. [1]
4. Claims that the pavilion was a royal speech platform are interpretations, not established facts used in this narration. [2]
