VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Vedanthangal Bird Sanctuary

1. The Chengalpattu district administration identifies Vedanthangal as a bird sanctuary. [1]
2. Its wetland and partly submerged trees provide a setting used by waterbirds for nesting. [1]
3. The district page lists birds including cormorants, herons, egrets, storks and spoonbills. [1]
4. Bird numbers and sightings vary with season and conditions; no sighting is guaranteed.
