Meenakshi Temple, Madurai — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The twin shrines are dedicated to Meenakshi and Sundareswarar.
2. The complex developed under several dynasties.
3. Major expansion took place under Thirumalai Nayak in the seventeenth century.
4. Sculpture-covered gateways and the Thousand Pillar Hall are important features.
5. The temple remains an active religious institution.
