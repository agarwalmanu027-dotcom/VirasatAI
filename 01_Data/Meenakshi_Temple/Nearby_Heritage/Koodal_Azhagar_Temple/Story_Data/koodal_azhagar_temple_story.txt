Koodal Azhagar Temple — story content

Koodal Azhagar Temple offers a Vaishnavite chapter in Madurai's sacred history. Dedicated to Vishnu, it is one of the one hundred and eight Divya Desams celebrated in the Tamil devotional tradition.

Its Dravidian architecture includes a gateway tower and spaces enclosed within a granite boundary. The temple's history reflects contributions associated with Pandya, Vijayanagara and Nayak rulers, rather than a single moment of construction.

Look at the way the entrance prepares you for the shrines beyond it. Religious buildings organise movement as well as display sculpture, guiding visitors from a busy city into a place of devotion.

The temple remains part of continuing worship, so follow local customs and access rules. Koodal Azhagar helps show that Madurai's heritage includes several important religious traditions, each with its own architecture, poetry and community of devotees.
