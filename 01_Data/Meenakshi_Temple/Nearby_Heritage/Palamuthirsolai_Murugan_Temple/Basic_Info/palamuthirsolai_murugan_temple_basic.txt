Overview
Pazhamudircholai is a hill shrine recognised as one of Murugan's six sacred abodes.

Identity and scope notes
Spelling variants Palamuthirsolai, Palamudirsolai and Pazhamudircholai refer to this hill shrine. It is distinct from the Vishnu temple below.
