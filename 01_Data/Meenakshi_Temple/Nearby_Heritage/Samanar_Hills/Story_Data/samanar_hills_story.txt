Samanar Hills — story content

Samanar Hills, also called Samanar Malai, preserve an important Jain chapter near Madurai. In the rocky setting around Keelakuyilkudi, sculptures and inscriptions make the hill a place to read as well as to view.

Two notable areas are Settipodavu and Pechchipallam. Their carved figures include Jain spiritual teachers, while inscriptions record people connected with the religious community. These details bring human names and support into the story of a sacred landscape.

The carvings are much smaller than the hill that carries them. Slow observation helps their forms emerge from the rock surface.

Stay on suitable routes and leave inscriptions untouched. Samanar Hills show how faith was preserved in places of retreat beyond the city's busy streets. Here, a natural formation became a lasting record of devotion, artistic work and the communities that sustained it.
