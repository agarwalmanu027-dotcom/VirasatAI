Vandiyur Mariamman Teppakulam — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Mariamman Teppakulam is a large square tank.
2. The district account associates its construction with Thirumalai Nayak.
3. It is used for a float festival involving Meenakshi and Sundareswarar.
4. A Mariamman temple stands on the tank's northern side.
