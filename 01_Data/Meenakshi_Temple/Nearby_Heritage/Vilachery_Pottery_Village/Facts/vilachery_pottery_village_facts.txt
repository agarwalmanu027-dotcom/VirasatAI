Vilachery Pottery Village — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Vilachery is known for doll-making and pottery.
2. DHAN Foundation identifies it as an intangible-heritage village.
3. Terracotta horses are produced for regional Ayyanar temple traditions.
4. Heritage lies in continuing skills as well as finished objects.
