VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Mehrangarh Fort, Jodhpur

1. Rao Jodha founded Mehrangarh in 1459. [1]
2. The fort and palaces developed over several centuries, with later rulers altering earlier buildings. [1]
3. Major building phases include the reigns of Maldeo and Ajit Singh. [1]
4. Museum galleries include paintings, textiles, arms, palanquins and elephant howdahs. [2]
