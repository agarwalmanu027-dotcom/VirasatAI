VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Balsamand Lake

1. The Ministry of Tourism identifies Balsamand as an artificial lake built to meet Mandore's water needs. [1]
2. The official account dates the reservoir to 1159 and associates it with Balak Rao Parihar. [1]
3. The lake setting includes gardens and a red sandstone summer palace with hospitality use. [1]
4. Current visitor access to private grounds must be checked; no public access is guaranteed.
