VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Jaswant Thada

1. The Mehrangarh Museum Trust identifies Jaswant Thada as a marble memorial to Maharaja Jaswant Singh II. [1]
2. It was commissioned by his son Maharaja Sardar Singh; the trust records completion in 1906. [1]
3. The principal hall has a temple-like form and a continuing ancestor-remembrance context. [1]
4. The narration describes a memorial, without asserting a burial beneath it.
