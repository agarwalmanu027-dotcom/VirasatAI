VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Machia Biological Park

1. Central Zoo Authority records identify Machia Biological Park as Jodhpur Zoo in Rajasthan. [1]
2. The CZA archive contains the park's institutional annual reports. [1,2]
3. The 2021–22 inventory is historical evidence, not a guarantee of animals displayed today. [3]
4. Machia Biological Park must not be confused with an unrestricted wildlife safari or a national park.
