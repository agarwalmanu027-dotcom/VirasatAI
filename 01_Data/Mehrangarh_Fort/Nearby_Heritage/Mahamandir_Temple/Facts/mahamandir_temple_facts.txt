VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Mahamandir Temple

1. Official tourism material identifies Mahamandir as a nineteenth-century temple dedicated to Shiva. [1]
2. The temple is known for eighty-four carved pillars and imagery of yoga postures. [1,2]
3. Conflicting patron names in promotional accounts are not repeated in the narration.
