VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Osian Temples

1. Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation identifies Osian as a destination with Jain and Vaishnava temples dating broadly to the eighth–eleventh centuries. [1]
2. State protected-monument records list named Osian temples including Harihar and other Hindu and Jain shrines. [2]
3. Osian should be described as a temple destination, not as one single building.
