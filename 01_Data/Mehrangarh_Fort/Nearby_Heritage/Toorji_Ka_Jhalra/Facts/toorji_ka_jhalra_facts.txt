Toorji Ka Jhalra

1. Official tourism material identifies Toorji Ka Jhalra as an eighteenth-century sandstone stepwell. [1,2]
2. Its stepped form provided access to stored water at changing levels. [1]
3. Exact founding year and competing name-origin stories are not asserted here. [1,2]
4. Access to lower steps depends on current instructions and conditions.
