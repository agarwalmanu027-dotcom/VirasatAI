Mysore Palace, Mysuru — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The present palace was built between 1897 and 1912.
2. Henry Irwin designed its Indo-Saracenic architecture.
3. The three-storey building has domed towers.
4. The Durbar Hall has decorated ceilings and sculpted pillars.
5. The Kalyanamantapa contains patterned flooring and stained glass.
