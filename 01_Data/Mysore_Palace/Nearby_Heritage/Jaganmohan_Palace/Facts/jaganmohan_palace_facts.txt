Jaganmohan Palace — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Jaganmohan Palace was built in 1861 by Mummadi Krishnaraja Wodeyar.
2. The entrance includes carvings representing Vishnu's ten incarnations.
3. The palace houses the Jayachamarajendra art collection.
4. Its paintings include works associated with Raja Ravi Varma and traditional Mysore painting.
