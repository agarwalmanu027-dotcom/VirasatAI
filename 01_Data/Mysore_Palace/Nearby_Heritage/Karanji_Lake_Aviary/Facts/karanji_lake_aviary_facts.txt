Karanji Lake Aviary — verified facts
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Karanji Lake Nature Park is managed by Mysuru Zoo.
2. A walk-through aviary is one of its facilities.
3. The zoo describes restoration from a waste-affected site to an aquatic ecosystem.
4. The park also includes a butterfly park and bird-watch tower.
