Kukkarahalli Lake — story content

Kukkarahalli Lake introduces a quieter part of Mysuru's heritage. Close to the University of Mysore, its water and surrounding trees are part of a landscape used for learning, walking and observing nature.

The lake is more than a pleasant view. University records describe its role in supporting vegetation and replenishing groundwater. Its setting also provides habitat for birds and aquatic life, reminding us that a city's history includes the natural systems that sustain it.

From the shore, notice how water, trees and nearby campus buildings share the same space. Crawford Hall and the Manasagangotri campus connect this environment with Mysuru's educational life.

A visit here invites slow observation. Listen to birds, watch the surface of the water and keep the surroundings free of waste. Kukkarahalli helps explain why protecting heritage also means caring for the everyday landscapes that people depend on.
