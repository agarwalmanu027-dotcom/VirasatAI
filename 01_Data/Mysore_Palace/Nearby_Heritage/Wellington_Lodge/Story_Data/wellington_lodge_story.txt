Wellington Lodge — story content

Wellington Lodge, also described as Wellington House in museum records, links Mysuru's architectural heritage with the study of people and culture. The historic building on Irwin Road is associated with the southern regional centre of the Indira Gandhi Rashtriya Manav Sangrahalaya.

Here, a heritage building becomes a setting for learning about communities, their practices and their relationship with the environment. Exhibitions connected with the institution examine cultural diversity through objects and ways of life.

This makes the visit different from viewing a royal hall. The building itself is one part of the story, while the cultures presented inside add another.

Think about the everyday objects a community uses and the skills needed to make them. Wellington Lodge encourages that kind of attention. It shows how a historic structure can continue to serve a public purpose by supporting understanding of India's many cultural traditions.
