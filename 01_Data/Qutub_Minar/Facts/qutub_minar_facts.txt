VERIFIED FACTS — Qutub Minar, Delhi
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Qutb Minar and its Monuments complex was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1993.
2. The tower is about 72.5 metres high and has carved sandstone surfaces and projecting balconies.
3. Construction began under Qutb-ud-din Aibak and continued under later rulers, including Iltutmish.
4. The complex includes Quwwat-ul-Islam Mosque, Iltutmish's Tomb, Alai Darwaza, the Iron Pillar and the unfinished Alai Minar.
