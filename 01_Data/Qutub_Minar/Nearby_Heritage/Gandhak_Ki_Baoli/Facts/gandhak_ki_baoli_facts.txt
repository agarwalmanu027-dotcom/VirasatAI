VERIFIED FACTS — Gandhak Ki Baoli
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Gandhak Ki Baoli is a stepwell in Mehrauli associated with Iltutmish.
2. INTACH records its association with the Sufi saint Qutbuddin Bakhtiyar Kaki.
3. The stepped structure includes a circular well; its name is associated with sulphur-smelling water.

Historical water descriptions are not a statement about current water quality. No medicinal or drinking-water claims are made.
