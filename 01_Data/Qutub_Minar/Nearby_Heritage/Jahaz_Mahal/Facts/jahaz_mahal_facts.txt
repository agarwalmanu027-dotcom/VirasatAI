VERIFIED FACTS — Jahaz Mahal
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Jahaz Mahal stands beside the Hauz-i-Shamsi reservoir in Mehrauli.
2. Delhi Tourism associates it with the Lodi period.
3. Its popular name means Ship Palace and is linked to the appearance of its reflection in water.
4. Its architectural features include chhatri pavilions.

Original function is debated, so inn, pleasure palace and mosque interpretations are not stated as settled facts.
