English master narrative
At Rajon Ki Baoli, the architecture leads your eye downward. This early sixteenth-century stepwell in Mehrauli Archaeological Park was designed around access to water.

Its name refers to masons, known as rajmistris. Steps descend through levels, with arcades and rooms along the sides. The structure served as a reservoir and also offered shaded places away from the summer heat.

Notice how practical needs produced a carefully ordered design. The repeated arches and descending steps create a sense of depth, while the rooms show that a water structure could have more than one use. Rajon Ki Baoli invites us to think about the daily life of an older city. Water was essential, and providing it could also create a place where architecture, shelter and community life met.
