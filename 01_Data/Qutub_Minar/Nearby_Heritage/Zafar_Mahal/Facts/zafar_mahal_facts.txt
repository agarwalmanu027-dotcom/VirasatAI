VERIFIED FACTS — Zafar Mahal
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Zafar Mahal in Mehrauli was originally built under Akbar II.
2. Its gateway was rebuilt under Bahadur Shah II, known as Bahadur Shah Zafar.
3. The palace uses red sandstone and marble and includes arcades and rooms.

This is the Mehrauli palace, not the pavilion named Zafar Mahal inside Red Fort.
