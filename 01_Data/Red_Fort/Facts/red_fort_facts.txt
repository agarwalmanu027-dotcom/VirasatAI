VERIFIED FACTS — Red Fort, Delhi
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Shah Jahan built the Red Fort as the palace fort of his capital Shahjahanabad.
2. Red-sandstone defensive walls enclose a palace layout with pavilions, halls and gardens.
3. A water channel called Nahr-i-Behisht connected the private apartments.
4. The UNESCO-listed complex includes the older Salimgarh Fort and was inscribed in 2007.
5. The fort is associated with India's Independence Day celebrations.
