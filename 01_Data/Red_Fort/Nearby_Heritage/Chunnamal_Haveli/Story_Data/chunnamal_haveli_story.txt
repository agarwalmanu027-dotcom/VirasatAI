English master narrative
Chunnamal Haveli introduces the merchant household into Old Delhi's history. Located in Katra Neel, the mansion is associated with Lala Chunnamal and a family whose trade included textiles and brocade.

A haveli was more than an impressive façade. It was part of a residential world connected with business, family life and the surrounding neighbourhood. Looking at such a building helps balance a heritage walk often centred on emperors and religious monuments.

Observe the exterior details without assuming that private spaces are open to visitors. The relationship between the house and the busy street is itself revealing. Chunnamal Haveli shows how trade could shape the built environment of Chandni Chowk. It preserves the memory of a prosperous household within a city where homes, markets and social life were closely linked.
