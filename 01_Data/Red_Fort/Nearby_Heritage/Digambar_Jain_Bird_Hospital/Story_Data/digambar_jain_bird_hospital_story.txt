English master narrative
The Digambar Jain Bird Hospital adds care for living creatures to a heritage journey through Old Delhi. It is associated with the Shri Digambar Jain Lal Mandir complex opposite the Red Fort.

Delhi Tourism describes the hospital as an avian veterinary institution in a separate building behind the main temple. This distinction matters: the hospital is connected with the religious site, but it has its own practical purpose.

Here, the story is about an idea expressed through service. Heritage can include institutions that connect belief with everyday care, alongside architecture and objects from the past. Observe only where visitors are allowed, and give staff and animals the quiet they need. The bird hospital encourages us to see the neighbourhood through compassion, adding another dimension to the cultural life surrounding the Red Fort.
