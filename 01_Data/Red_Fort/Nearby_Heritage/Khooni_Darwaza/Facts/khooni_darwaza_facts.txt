VERIFIED FACTS — Khooni Darwaza
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Khooni Darwaza is also documented as Lal Darwaza and Kabuli Darwaza.
2. It belongs to the city defences associated with Sher Shah's sixteenth-century Delhi.
3. The gateway is distinct from the Delhi Gate of Shahjahanabad.

Violent anecdotes and ghost narratives are not narrated. The gateway is not labelled an original Red Fort entrance.
