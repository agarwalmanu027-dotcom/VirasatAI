English master narrative
Khooni Darwaza is a gateway with an identity older than Shahjahanabad. Also documented as Lal Darwaza and Kabuli Darwaza, it is connected with the city defences of Sher Shah's sixteenth-century Delhi.

Its position near Delhi Gate can cause confusion, but the two gateways are different monuments. Keeping that distinction clear helps us read the city's historical layers more accurately.

Look at the structure as a piece of urban planning. A gateway marked a passage through a boundary and influenced the movement of people into and out of a city. The surviving building therefore preserves a practical role as well as a memorable name. Khooni Darwaza invites us to look beyond dramatic stories and consider how older roads, defences and later neighbourhoods became part of the same modern landscape.

इसे शहर की योजना का हिस्सा मानकर देखें। दरवाज़ा किसी सीमा से गुजरने का स्थान था और लोगों के आने-जाने को प्रभावित करता था। बची हुई इमारत, याद रहने वाले नाम के साथ एक व्यावहारिक भूमिका भी सुरक्षित रखती है। खूनी दरवाज़ा हमें नाटकीय कथाओं से आगे बढ़कर सोचने के लिए प्रेरित करता है कि पुरानी सड़कें, सुरक्षा व्यवस्था और बाद की बस्तियाँ आधुनिक शहर में कैसे मिल गईं।
