English master narrative
St. James' Church adds a nineteenth-century chapter to the Kashmiri Gate area. Commissioned by Colonel James Skinner and consecrated in eighteen thirty-six, it is also known as Skinner's Church.

Its cross-shaped plan, entrance porticoes and central dome give it a distinctive form. Stained glass introduces another way for light to become part of an interior. Look at the proportions and the relationship between the enclosed church and its grounds.

The building remains connected with worship, so heritage observation should respect the life of the congregation. Its presence also helps explain the changing character of Delhi during the colonial period. St. James' offers a different architectural language within the wider Old Delhi landscape, reminding us that the city's history was shaped by several communities and by many kinds of sacred space.
