Overview
The interpreted selected entry is the Sunehri Masjid near Red Fort, an ASI-listed monument.

Identity and scope
Delhi has several mosques called Sunehri Masjid. This content explicitly uses the one near Red Fort/Delhi Gate; do not mix its images/history with Roshan-ud-Daula's mosque near Sis Ganj or Sunehri Bagh mosque. Royal relationship wording conflicts between records and is omitted.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
