VERIFIED FACTS — Sunehri Masjid
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The interpreted selected entry is the Sunehri Masjid near Red Fort, an ASI-listed monument.
2. It dates to 1751 and is associated with Qudsia Begum.
3. Its architectural features include three domes and two minarets.

Delhi has several mosques called Sunehri Masjid. This content explicitly uses the one near Red Fort/Delhi Gate; do not mix its images/history with Roshan-ud-Daula's mosque near Sis Ganj or Sunehri Bagh mosque. Royal relationship wording conflicts between records and is omitted.
