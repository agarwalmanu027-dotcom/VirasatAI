Overview
Zeenat-ul-Masajid is a mosque in Daryaganj associated with Zinat-un-Nisa, Aurangzeb's daughter.

Identity and scope
Historical publications disagree on the exact year and contain conversion errors, so the narration gives the Mughal-period association without an exact year. Do not confuse the mosque's patron with Bahadur Shah Zafar's wife Zeenat Mahal.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
