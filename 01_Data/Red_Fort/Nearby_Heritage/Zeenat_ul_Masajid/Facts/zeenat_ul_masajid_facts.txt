VERIFIED FACTS — Zeenat-ul-Masajid
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Zeenat-ul-Masajid is a mosque in Daryaganj associated with Zinat-un-Nisa, Aurangzeb's daughter.
2. Historical ASI accounts describe red-sandstone construction and striped marble domes.
3. The prayer façade is accompanied by minarets and a courtyard.

Historical publications disagree on the exact year and contain conversion errors, so the narration gives the Mughal-period association without an exact year. Do not confuse the mosque's patron with Bahadur Shah Zafar's wife Zeenat Mahal.
