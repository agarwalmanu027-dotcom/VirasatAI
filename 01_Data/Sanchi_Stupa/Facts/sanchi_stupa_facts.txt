VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Sanchi Stupa, Madhya Pradesh

1. The sacred complex began under Emperor Ashoka in the third century BCE. [1]
2. The Great Stupa developed through later enlargements and additions. [1]
3. Carved gateways use Buddhist symbols and Jataka narratives. [1]
4. The property includes stupas, temples, monasteries and pillars. [1,2]
5. UNESCO inscribed the Buddhist Monuments at Sanchi in 1989. [1]
