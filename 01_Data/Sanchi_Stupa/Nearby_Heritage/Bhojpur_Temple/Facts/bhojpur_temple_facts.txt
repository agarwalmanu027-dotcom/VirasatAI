Bhojpur Temple

1. Bhojeshwar Temple at Bhojpur is dedicated to Shiva. [1]
2. The unfinished temple is associated with the eleventh-century ruler Raja Bhoj. [1]
3. Its large Shiva lingam is carved from a single stone. [1]
4. ASI lists the temple and ancient remains at Bhojpur as protected. [2]
