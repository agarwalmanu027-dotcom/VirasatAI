VIRASATAI — IMPORTANT FACTS
Bijamandal

1. This entry concerns Bijamandal in Vidisha city, not Bijamandal near Khajuraho or the namesake at Udaipur in Vidisha district. [1,2]
2. Madhya Pradesh Tourism describes surviving temple and mosque remains here. [1]
3. The National Monuments Authority identifies the protected monument as Bijamandal Mosque, Bhilsa, Vidisha. [2]
4. ASI's antiquity inventory records architectural fragments from Bijamandal, Vidisha. [3]
