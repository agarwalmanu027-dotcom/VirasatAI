1. The pillar stands at Besnagar near Vidisha. [1]
2. Its inscription identifies Heliodorus, son of Dion, as an envoy of the Indo-Greek king Antialkidas. [1]
3. It was dedicated in honour of Vasudeva and is locally called Khamba Baba. [1,2]
