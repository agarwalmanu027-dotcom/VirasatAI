Neelkantheshwar Temple

1. The temple is at Udaipur in Vidisha district, Madhya Pradesh. [1,2]
2. It is associated with the Paramara ruler Udayaditya. [1]
3. Its inscriptions record construction between 1059 and 1080. [1]
4. The historical census account identifies a shrine, hall and entrance porches. [2]
