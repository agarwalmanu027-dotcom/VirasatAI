Satdhara Stupas

1. Satdhara is a Buddhist stupa site overlooking the Halali river landscape. [1]
2. ASI lists stupas and other remains at Satdhara under Murlikhedi in Raisen district. [2]
3. The site is distinct from Satdhara Falls in Himachal Pradesh. [1,2]
