Udayagiri Caves

1. These are the Udayagiri caves near Vidisha, not the similarly named Odisha caves. [1,2]
2. The site contains Gupta-period rock carvings and inscriptions. [1]
3. Madhya Pradesh Tourism describes twenty caves carved during the fourth and fifth centuries. [2]
