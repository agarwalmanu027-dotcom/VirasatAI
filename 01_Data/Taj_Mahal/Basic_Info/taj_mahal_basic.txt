VIRASATAI — TAJ MAHAL BASIC INFORMATION

Monument Name: Taj Mahal
Location: Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India
River: Yamuna River
Period: Mughal Period
Commissioned By: Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan
Dedicated To: Mumtaz Mahal
UNESCO World Heritage Site: Yes
UNESCO Inscription Year: 1983
UNESCO World Heritage Dossier: 252

Construction:
- Construction began in 1632, according to UNESCO.
- The principal mausoleum was completed in 1648.
- Other major complex components were completed subsequently, around 1653.

Main Architectural Features:
- White marble mausoleum
- Large central dome
- Four minarets
- Symmetrical design
- Marble jali (lattice screens)
- Pietra dura inlay
- Arabic calligraphy
- Mughal-style formal garden
- Mosque on the western side
- Guest House (Mihman Khana) on the eastern side
- Yamuna riverfront setting

Visitor Information:
- The Taj Mahal is generally closed to visitors on Fridays.
- Normal visiting hours are generally from 30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset.
- Visitor rules and timings can change, so the app should verify current information from official sources.
