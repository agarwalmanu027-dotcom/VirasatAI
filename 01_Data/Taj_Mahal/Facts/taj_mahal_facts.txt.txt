1. BASIC FACTS
========================================

Monument Name:
Taj Mahal

Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India

Yamuna River

Period:
Mughal Period

Commissioned By:
Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan

Dedicated To:
Mumtaz Mahal

Type:
Mausoleum and monumental complex

UNESCO World Heritage Site:
Yes

UNESCO Inscription:
1983

UNESCO World Heritage Dossier:
252


2. HISTORICAL FACTS
========================================

- Mumtaz Mahal died in 1631.

- Shah Jahan commissioned the Taj Mahal in her memory.

- According to UNESCO, construction of the Taj Mahal began
  in 1632.

- The principal mausoleum was completed in 1648.

- The mosque, guest house, main southern gateway, outer
  courtyard and cloisters were completed subsequently,
  with the larger complex completed around 1653.

- The Taj Mahal was created during the Mughal period under
  Emperor Shah Jahan.

- Artisans and craftsmen were brought from different parts
  of the Mughal Empire, Central Asia and Iran.


3. ARCHITECTURAL FACTS
========================================

- The Taj Mahal is an important achievement of
  Indo-Islamic / Mughal architecture.

- Its design combines Persian, Indian and Islamic
  architectural traditions.

- The complex is strongly based on geometry and symmetry.

- The main mausoleum is faced with white marble.

- A large central dome dominates the main mausoleum.

- Four minarets stand at the corners of the main marble
  platform.

- The main mausoleum has a highly symmetrical composition.

- The mosque is located on the western side of the
  mausoleum.

- The Mihman Khana (Guest House) is located on the
  eastern side.

- The mosque and guest house help create the balanced
  composition of the complex.


4. MARBLE AND MATERIALS
========================================

- White marble is the dominant material of the main
  mausoleum.

- The official Taj Mahal website identifies Makrana,
  Rajasthan, as the source of the famous white marble.

- Red sandstone is used in important surrounding
  structures such as the mosque and guest house.

- Different decorative stones were used for ornamental
  inlay work.


5. PIETRA DURA
========================================

- Pietra dura is an important decorative technique used
  at the Taj Mahal.

- It involves inlaying pieces of stone into marble.

- Floral and plant-inspired designs are found in the
  decorative work.

- The technique demonstrates the high level of craftsmanship
  used in the monument.


6. MARBLE JALI
========================================

- Marble jali means decorative lattice work carved from
  marble.

- Ornamental marble lattice screens are used around the
  cenotaph area.

- The screens allow light to enter.

- They create patterns of light and shadow.

- The jali work demonstrates the skill of the craftsmen.


7. CALLIGRAPHY
========================================

- Arabic calligraphy is an important decorative feature
  of the Taj Mahal.

- Calligraphic inscriptions appear on architectural
  surfaces of the monument.

- Amanat Khan of Shiraz is identified in official Taj Mahal
  material as the chief calligrapher.


8. CENOTAPHS
========================================

- The central chamber contains the cenotaphs associated
  with Mumtaz Mahal and Shah Jahan.

- The cenotaphs are richly decorated.

- The actual burial area is located below the main
  cenotaph level.

- The cenotaph arrangement is an important part of the
  interior design.


9. GARDEN
========================================

- The Taj Mahal complex contains a formal Mughal-style
  garden.

- The garden includes pathways, water channels and
  reflecting pools.

- The garden is organized according to geometric principles.

- The relationship between the garden, water and mausoleum
  is an important part of the monument's visual design.


10. YAMUNA RIVER
========================================

- The Taj Mahal stands on the right bank of the Yamuna River.

- The river is an important part of the historical and
  architectural setting of the monument.

- The Taj Mahal has a significant riverfront architectural
  relationship.


11. SYMMETRY
========================================

- Symmetry is one of the most recognizable characteristics
  of the Taj Mahal.

- The complex is organized around major central axes.

- The main mausoleum has strong bilateral symmetry.

- The mosque and guest house form complementary elements
  on opposite sides of the mausoleum.

- Geometric relationships create a strong sense of balance
  and harmony.


12. CRAFTSMANSHIP
========================================

- The Taj Mahal required many specialized craftsmen.

- UNESCO mentions masons, stone-cutters, inlayers, carvers,
  painters, calligraphers, dome builders and other artisans.

- Craftsmen came from across the Mughal Empire and from
  Central Asia and Iran.

- The monument combines structural architecture with
  highly detailed decorative craftsmanship.


13. ARCHITECTS
========================================

- The history of the Taj Mahal's design involves a team
  of architects and specialists.

- UNESCO identifies Ustad-Ahmad Lahori as the main architect.

- The official Taj Mahal website also identifies several
  other architects and specialists associated with the
  construction.

- Because historical sources differ in how individual roles
  are described, the app should avoid claiming that only
  one person was responsible for the entire monument.


14. CONSERVATION HISTORY
========================================

- The Taj Mahal required restoration and conservation
  during its history.

- The official Taj Mahal history material describes
  deterioration by the late 19th century.

- It also describes damage during the period of the
  1857 Indian Rebellion.

- A major restoration project was carried out under
  British Viceroy Lord Curzon.

- The restoration was completed in 1908.

- The garden was also remodeled during this period.


15. UNESCO FACTS
========================================

- The Taj Mahal was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage
  List in 1983.

- UNESCO recognizes it as an outstanding achievement of
  Indo-Islamic architecture.

- The World Heritage property covers nearly 17 hectares.

- The monument is located in Agra District, Uttar Pradesh.

- UNESCO World Heritage Dossier number:
  252


16. VISITOR FACTS
========================================

According to the official Taj Mahal website:

- The Taj Mahal is generally closed to visitors on Fridays.

- On other days, visiting hours are generally from
  30 minutes before sunrise to 30 minutes after sunset.

- Drone cameras are prohibited in and around the Taj Mahal.

- Food items are not allowed inside the Taj Mahal.

IMPORTANT:
Visitor timings and rules may change. VirasatAI should
verify current visitor information from official sources
before displaying it to users.
