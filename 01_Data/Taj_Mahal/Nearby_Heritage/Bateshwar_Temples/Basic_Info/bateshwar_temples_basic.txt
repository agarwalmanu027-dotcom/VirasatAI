Overview
This entry refers to the Bateshwar temples beside the Yamuna in Agra district.

Identity and scope
Do not confuse this with the Bateshwar archaeological temple group in Morena, Madhya Pradesh. Official pages give different temple counts; no exact count is narrated. It is a regional excursion, not an assumed walkable stop from the Taj.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
