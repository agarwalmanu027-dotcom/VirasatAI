VERIFIED FACTS — Bateshwar Temples
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. This entry refers to the Bateshwar temples beside the Yamuna in Agra district.
2. Bateshwar is a pilgrimage town with a chain of Hindu temples.
3. The annual Bateshwar fair combines religious gatherings and a livestock fair.

Do not confuse this with the Bateshwar archaeological temple group in Morena, Madhya Pradesh. Official pages give different temple counts; no exact count is narrated. It is a regional excursion, not an assumed walkable stop from the Taj.
