English master narrative
Jaswant Singh Ki Chhatri introduces a Rajput memorial into Agra's riverside heritage. The site is associated with the cremation of Amar Singh Rathore and takes its name from his brother, Jaswant Singh.

Its carved columns and stone screens deserve a close look. These features connect the memorial to the wider architectural world of seventeenth-century Agra, where different patrons worked with related materials and building traditions.

A chhatri is a memorial pavilion, and this one invites reflection as well as observation. Notice how solid stone becomes a lighter surface through openings and carving. The monument also expands our understanding of the city. Agra's heritage includes more than the buildings of Mughal emperors. Smaller memorials preserve other families and communities, each adding a distinct voice to the historical landscape.
