VERIFIED FACTS — Kanch Mahal
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Kanch Mahal stands near the southeast corner of the Akbar's Tomb enclosure at Sikandra.
2. It is a two-storeyed Mughal-period building associated with Jahangir.
3. Glazed tiles and coloured stone decoration contributed to its ornamental character.

This entry is Kanch Mahal at Sikandra. Do not confuse it with similarly named rooms in other palaces. Specific original-use claims are omitted.
