English master narrative
Before the Taj Mahal became Agra's most famous garden setting, Babur introduced an earlier Mughal landscape here. Ram Bagh was laid out around fifteen twenty-eight and remains an important part of the city's garden heritage.

The site brings together terraces, water channels and pavilions beside the Yamuna. These features show how a garden could be carefully designed as a place of movement, rest and views. Follow the arrangement of paths and notice how built structures meet open space.

Ram Bagh helps place the Taj Mahal in a longer story. Mughal garden design developed over generations, rather than appearing all at once. Here, that early chapter becomes easier to imagine. The most revealing details may be the layout itself: where a path leads, how a terrace rises and how the river shapes the setting.
