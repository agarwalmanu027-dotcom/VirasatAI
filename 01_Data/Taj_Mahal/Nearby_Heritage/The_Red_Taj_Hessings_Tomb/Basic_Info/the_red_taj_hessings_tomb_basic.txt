Overview
The Red Taj is the tomb of Colonel John William Hessing in Agra's Roman Catholic cemetery.

Identity and scope
The historic register's table has a conflicting death-year label while its inscription records 1803. Avoid an exact death date in narration. Do not describe the entire cemetery as the Red Taj.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
