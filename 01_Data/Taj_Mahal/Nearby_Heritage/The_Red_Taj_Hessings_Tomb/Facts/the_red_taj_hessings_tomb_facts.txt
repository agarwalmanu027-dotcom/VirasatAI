VERIFIED FACTS — The Red Taj (Hessing's Tomb)
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Red Taj is the tomb of Colonel John William Hessing in Agra's Roman Catholic cemetery.
2. Hessing was born in Utrecht in the Netherlands and served Indian rulers.
3. The red-sandstone mausoleum uses a dome, arched niches, screens and corner pavilions.

The historic register's table has a conflicting death-year label while its inscription records 1803. Avoid an exact death date in narration. Do not describe the entire cemetery as the Red Taj.
