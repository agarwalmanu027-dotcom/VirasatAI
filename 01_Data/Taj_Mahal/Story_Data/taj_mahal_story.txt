VIRASATAI — TAJ MAHAL STORY DATA

PURPOSE:
Key facts needed to create an engaging 1–2 minute storytelling script.

1. THE BEGINNING
Shah Jahan commissioned the Taj Mahal in memory of Mumtaz Mahal, who died in 1631.

2. WHEN IT WAS BUILT
According to UNESCO, construction began in 1632. The principal mausoleum was completed in 1648, while other parts of the complex were completed subsequently.

3. LOCATION
The Taj Mahal stands on the right bank of the Yamuna River in Agra, Uttar Pradesh.

4. THE WHITE MARBLE
The main mausoleum is faced with white marble. The official Taj Mahal website identifies Makrana in Rajasthan as the source of the famous white marble.

5. SYMMETRY
Symmetry is one of the defining characteristics of the Taj Mahal. Its buildings, garden and major architectural elements are arranged around strong geometric axes.

6. THE DOME AND MINARETS
The central mausoleum is dominated by a large white marble dome. Four minarets stand at the corners of the main marble platform.

7. PIETRA DURA
The monument contains detailed pietra dura decoration, in which stone pieces are inlaid into marble to create decorative floral and plant-inspired designs.

8. MARBLE JALI
Ornamental marble lattice screens surround the cenotaph area. They allow light to enter and create patterns of light and shadow.

9. CALLIGRAPHY
Arabic calligraphy forms an important part of the monument's decoration. Amanat Khan of Shiraz is identified in the official Taj Mahal material as the chief calligrapher.

10. MOSQUE, GUEST HOUSE AND GARDEN
A mosque stands to the west of the mausoleum and a guest house stands to the east, helping create the balanced composition. The complex also contains a formal Mughal-style garden with pathways, water channels and reflecting pools.

11. CRAFTSMANSHIP
The Taj Mahal was created through the work of many specialized artisans and craftsmen. UNESCO notes that artisans came from across the Mughal Empire and from Central Asia and Iran.

12. WORLD HERITAGE
The Taj Mahal was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List in 1983 and is recognized as an outstanding achievement of Indo-Islamic architecture.

STORYTELLING ORDER:
Hook → Mumtaz Mahal → Shah Jahan → Construction → Yamuna → White marble → Symmetry → Dome & minarets → Pietra dura/jali → Garden → Craftsmanship → UNESCO → Closing.

SCRIPT RULE:
Do not include every fact in the final narration. Select the most interesting facts and connect them naturally into a story.
