Overview
Victoria Memorial was conceived after Queen Victoria's death in 1901, with Lord Curzon leading the proposal.

Identity and scope
Public opening in 1921 is not equated with completion of every part. Architecture and museum history are narrated with colonial context; no quotation from Curzon is reproduced.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
