VERIFIED FACTS — Victoria Memorial, Kolkata
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Victoria Memorial was conceived after Queen Victoria's death in 1901, with Lord Curzon leading the proposal.
2. William Emerson designed the building; Vincent Esch supervised work.
3. The monument opened to the public on 28 December 1921, with some construction completed later.
4. Its museum collections include paintings, photographs, manuscripts, objects and records relevant to Indian history.
5. The marble building is set in gardens on the Kolkata Maidan.

Public opening in 1921 is not equated with completion of every part. Architecture and museum history are narrated with colonial context; no quotation from Curzon is reproduced.
