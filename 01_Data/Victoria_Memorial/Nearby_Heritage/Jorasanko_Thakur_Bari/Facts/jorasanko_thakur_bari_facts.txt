VERIFIED FACTS — Jorasanko Thakur Bari
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Jorasanko Thakur Bari is Rabindranath Tagore's ancestral home.
2. Tagore was born here in 1861 and spent significant parts of his life here.
3. The site houses the Rabindra Bharati Museum.
4. The Tagore household is connected with literary and artistic developments, including Gaganendranath and Abanindranath Tagore.

University establishment dates vary between official pages and are omitted. Buildings within the historic family compound are not all assumed to survive.
