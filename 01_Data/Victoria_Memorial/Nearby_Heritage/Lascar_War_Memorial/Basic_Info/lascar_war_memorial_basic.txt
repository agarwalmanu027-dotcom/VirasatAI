Overview
The Lascar War Memorial commemorates 896 seamen of Eastern India who died at sea during the First World War.

Identity and scope
Lascars are described as seamen, not automatically navy soldiers. Access to naval premises is not promised. The exact unveiling official's title varies in the publication and is omitted.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
