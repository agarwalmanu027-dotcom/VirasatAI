VERIFIED FACTS — Lascar War Memorial
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. The Lascar War Memorial commemorates 896 seamen of Eastern India who died at sea during the First World War.
2. It was unveiled on 6 February 1924.
3. The tall four-sided monument includes maritime design references and Indian architectural details.
4. The monument lies in Kolkata's naval precinct near the southern Maidan.

Lascars are described as seamen, not automatically navy soldiers. Access to naval premises is not promised. The exact unveiling official's title varies in the publication and is omitted.
