Overview
Magen David, also spelled Maghen David, is a Jewish synagogue in Kolkata.

Identity and scope
Official sources disagree on the exact patron's name/gender and stylistic label; broad Ezra-family patronage is used. This is Kolkata's Magen David, distinct from the synagogue of the same name in Mumbai and from adjacent Neveh Shalom.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
