VERIFIED FACTS — Magen David Synagogue
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Magen David, also spelled Maghen David, is a Jewish synagogue in Kolkata.
2. It opened in 1884 and is associated with the Ezra family's patronage.
3. The building has a brick-red exterior, tall tower and richly decorated interior.
4. The interior includes a central nave, side aisles and a tebah, the platform used for readings and prayer.

Official sources disagree on the exact patron's name/gender and stylistic label; broad Ezra-family patronage is used. This is Kolkata's Magen David, distinct from the synagogue of the same name in Mumbai and from adjacent Neveh Shalom.
