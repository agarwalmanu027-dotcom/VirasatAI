VERIFIED FACTS — Mullick Ghat Flower Market
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Mullick Ghat Flower Market lies on the Kolkata side of the Hooghly waterfront near Howrah Bridge.
2. Research on the market documents flower growers, traders and vendors and their exchange networks.
3. The market is a working commercial place rather than an isolated architectural monument.
4. Its cultural significance includes the connection between flowers, livelihoods and urban demand.

No largest-in-Asia claim, fixed stall count, exact founding date or current operating layout is asserted. Current vendor spaces can change.
