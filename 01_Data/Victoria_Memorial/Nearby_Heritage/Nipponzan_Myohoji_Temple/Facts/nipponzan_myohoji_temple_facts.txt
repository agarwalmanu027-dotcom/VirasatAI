VERIFIED FACTS — Nipponzan Myohoji Temple
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Nipponzan Myohoji Temple is a Japanese Buddhist temple in Dhakuria, South Kolkata.
2. An India–Japan student conference documented a firsthand visit with the temple's priest and community.
3. The temple includes a Buddhist altar, Japanese calligraphy and a garden setting.
4. The Nipponzan Myohoji order is associated with Nichidatsu Fujii and a peace-oriented tradition.

The student report inaccurately describes Fujii as a personal disciple of the medieval Nichiren; that claim is omitted. Darjeeling Peace Pagoda is distinct and not substituted. Temple founding date and statue count are omitted.
