Prinsep Ghat

Place ID: victoria_memorial__prinsep_ghat
Location: Hooghly riverfront near Fort William, Kolkata, West Bengal
Type: Riverside commemorative pavilion and ghat
User-selected parent group: Victoria Memorial, Kolkata
Content checked on: 2026-10-07

Overview
Prinsep Ghat commemorates James Prinsep, a scholar and antiquarian.

Identity and scope
A tourism page describes Venetian Gothic style and a clock tower; these are excluded in favour of Asiatic Society documentation of the Palladian porch. Howrah Bridge and Vidyasagar Setu are not confused.

Visitor information
Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
