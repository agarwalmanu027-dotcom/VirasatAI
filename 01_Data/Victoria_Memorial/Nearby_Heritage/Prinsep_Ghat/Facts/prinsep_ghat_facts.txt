VERIFIED FACTS — Prinsep Ghat
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. Prinsep Ghat commemorates James Prinsep, a scholar and antiquarian.
2. Its Palladian or neoclassical porch was designed by W. Fitzgerald and erected in 1843.
3. It was funded through civic subscription and government support.
4. The monument forms part of Kolkata's Hooghly riverfront.

A tourism page describes Venetian Gothic style and a clock tower; these are excluded in favour of Asiatic Society documentation of the Palladian porch. Howrah Bridge and Vidyasagar Setu are not confused.
