VERIFIED FACTS — South Park Street Cemetery
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. South Park Street Cemetery dates to 1767.
2. Its tombs preserve aspects of Kolkata's eighteenth- and nineteenth-century society.
3. Sir William Jones and Henry Louis Vivian Derozio are among people buried here.
4. Monuments include obelisks, columns, domed forms and inscribed memorials.
5. The Christian Burial Board maintains the heritage cemetery.

Burials are not assumed to have stopped exactly in 1790. Ghost stories and sensational claims are excluded.
