English master narrative
South Park Street Cemetery preserves lives and memories within the history of Kolkata. Established in 1767, the burial ground contains monuments that reflect the city's eighteenth- and nineteenth-century society. Sir William Jones and the poet and teacher Henry Louis Vivian Derozio are among those remembered here.

The tombs vary in form. Obelisks, columns, domed structures and inscribed memorials show different ways of expressing remembrance. Look at the shapes, then consider the words and names they preserve. Architecture and inscription provide different kinds of evidence.

The Christian Burial Board's conservation work helps protect this historic setting. A cemetery requires care not only because its structures are old, but because it remains a place of human loss and memory.

Walk with respect. Beyond prominent names, the site invites us to think about the many individual lives behind the larger history of a city.
