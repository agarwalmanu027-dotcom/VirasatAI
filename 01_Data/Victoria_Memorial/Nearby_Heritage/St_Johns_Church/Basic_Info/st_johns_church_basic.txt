Overview
St. John's Church was completed in 1787 and designed by James Agg.

Identity and scope
This is Kolkata's St. John's Church, not the St. James' Church in Delhi. Superlative first-cathedral claims and disputed Job Charnock founder narrative are omitted.

Live opening hours, fees, photography rules and access arrangements are deliberately excluded. Confirm these directly with the site before visiting.
