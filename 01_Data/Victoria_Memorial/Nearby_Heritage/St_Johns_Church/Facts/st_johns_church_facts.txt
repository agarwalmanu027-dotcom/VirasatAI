VERIFIED FACTS — St. John's Church
Checked: 2026-10-07

1. St. John's Church was completed in 1787 and designed by James Agg.
2. Its neoclassical design was inspired by St. Martin-in-the-Fields in London.
3. The brick-and-stone church is also known as Pathure Girja, or Stone Church.
4. Columns, pediments, a steeple and memorial tablets are significant features.
5. It formerly served as Kolkata's Anglican cathedral before St. Paul's.

This is Kolkata's St. John's Church, not the St. James' Church in Delhi. Superlative first-cathedral claims and disputed Job Charnock founder narrative are omitted.
