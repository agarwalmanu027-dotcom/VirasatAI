Bhadra Maruti Temple in Khuldabad offers a distinctive image of Hanuman. The deity, also known as Maruti, is shown reclining rather than in the standing form familiar at many shrines.

A devotional legend connects this posture with King Bhadrasen, whose hymns in praise of Rama are said to have drawn Hanuman into a state of peaceful absorption. This is a sacred tradition explaining the image, rather than a dated historical event.

The unusual pose gives the shrine its individual identity. It encourages a different visual encounter with a deity widely associated with strength and devoted service.

Respect the temple's customs, ask before photographing worship and give devotees space. Bhadra Maruti adds a living Hindu sacred place to Khuldabad's wider heritage, showing how a memorable image and its remembered story can shape the character of a pilgrimage site.
