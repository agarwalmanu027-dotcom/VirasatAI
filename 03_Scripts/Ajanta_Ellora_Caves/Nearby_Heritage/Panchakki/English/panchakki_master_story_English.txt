Panchakki tells a heritage story through the movement of water. Its name identifies a water mill, set within the religious complex associated with Baba Shah Musafir in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar.

An underground channel brought water from a source in the hills. At the site, the water's fall supplied the force for milling. The system connects a distant source, carefully directed flow and a practical task at the end.

The mill is associated with grinding grain for people visiting the nearby religious institution. Engineering therefore served daily needs as well as creating a memorable architectural feature.

Observe the water system from permitted areas and do not enter channels. Panchakki encourages us to look beyond façades when exploring heritage. A building's importance can lie in the way it works, and in the relationship between knowledge, resources and the community it supported.
