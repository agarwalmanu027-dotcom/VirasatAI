Salim Ali Lake brings a natural landmark into the northern part of Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. It is named after the renowned ornithologist Salim Ali, whose work helped draw attention to India's birdlife.

The lake and its surrounding habitat support birds within an urban setting. Local and migratory species are part of its identity, although what you see will depend on the season and conditions.

Look at the edges as well as the open water. Vegetation, shallows and quieter areas can matter greatly to the creatures using the place. Birdwatching begins with patience and an awareness of habitat.

Keep a respectful distance and leave no litter. The lake shows why nature belongs in a city's heritage story. A remembered scientist's name connects with a living landscape, while the survival of that landscape depends on the care it receives.
