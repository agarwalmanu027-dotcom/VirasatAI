Amer Fort rises above the old town of Amer, bringing a palace and a fortified hill setting together. Also known as Amber Palace, it is part of the UNESCO-listed Hill Forts of Rajasthan.

The large walls suggest defence, but the spaces inside tell a story of court life. Courtyards, audience halls and gardens lead you through different parts of the palace. Each transition changes the scale of the experience, from a broad open space to a more closely decorated room.

One of the best-known interiors is the Sheesh Mahal, where mirror decoration makes the surfaces part of the attraction. Notice the small details as well as the overall effect. Craftsmanship is often most visible when you stop looking only at the size of a building.

Amer also shows the development of a shared Rajput-Mughal court style. UNESCO highlights the buildings and gardens added during the seventeenth century as an important expression of that exchange.

From the hill setting to the palace interiors, this complex combines power, practical planning and ornament. Its story is spread across many spaces. Follow their arrangement carefully, and Amer becomes more than a dramatic outline above Jaipur: it becomes a place where architecture shaped royal life.
