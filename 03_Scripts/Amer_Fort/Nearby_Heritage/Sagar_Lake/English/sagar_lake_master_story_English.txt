Behind Amer Fort, Sagar Lake adds water to the story of the hill settlement. Also known as Sagar Kund, it formed part of the area's historic water landscape.

Government planning records connect the lake with water supply for Amer and the adjoining forts. That role helps explain why a lake deserves attention alongside the palace walls. A fortified settlement needed resources as well as strong architecture.

Look at the surrounding terrain and consider how water could be collected within it. This Sagar Lake should be distinguished from Maota below Amer Fort and Man Sagar near Jal Mahal. Keeping those identities clear makes the heritage story more accurate. Sagar invites us to see the less visible systems behind a royal centre, where landscape and water helped support life on and around the hills.
