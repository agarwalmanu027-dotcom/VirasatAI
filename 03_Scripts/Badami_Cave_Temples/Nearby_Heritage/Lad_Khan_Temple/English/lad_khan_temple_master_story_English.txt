Lad Khan Temple is one of Aihole's distinctive early stone buildings. Its familiar name should not make you assume an Islamic origin for the temple. The architecture and the Hindu imagery described by Karnataka Tourism belong to a different context from the later name through which it is known.

Look at the relationship between the hall, its supports and the roof. A building can express sacred purpose through the arrangement of space as well as through sculpture. The temple's images draw on Shaiva, Vaishnava and Shakti traditions, encouraging attention to varied subjects rather than a single decorative theme.

Compare its shape with the nearby Durga Temple where access permits. Their differences make Aihole's architectural variety easier to appreciate. Follow site instructions and avoid leaning on old stonework. Lad Khan Temple offers a useful lesson in reading heritage carefully: names, surviving buildings and stories of later use may carry different layers of history, and should not be collapsed into one explanation.
