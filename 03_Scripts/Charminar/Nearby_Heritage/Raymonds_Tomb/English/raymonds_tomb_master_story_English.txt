Raymond's Tomb reveals a French connection within Hyderabad's history. The memorial remembers Michel Joachim Marie Raymond, a French general who served in the Nizam's military and is associated with the city's Gunfoundry.

The tomb occupies a hillock in the Moosarambagh and Malakpet area. Its tall black granite marker gives it a different appearance from Hyderabad's better-known domed tombs. The form directs attention upward, while the elevated setting makes location part of the memorial's character.

This place introduces the movement of people, skills and military knowledge across regions. Hyderabad's past included relationships that reached well beyond the Deccan, and Raymond's story is one example of those connections.

Take time to see the monument as a record of one person's place in a wider historical world. Small memorials can bring international relationships into a city's local landscape.
