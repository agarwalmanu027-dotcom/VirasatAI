Ballard Bunder Gatehouse belongs to the history of Mumbai as a working port. Its setting connects the waterfront with Ballard Estate, a business district planned close to the harbour. Here, heritage includes the routes and boundaries through which people and goods once moved.

A gatehouse is worth noticing because it marks a transition. It directs attention to the relationship between the street, the port and the sea beyond. The surrounding precinct shows how architecture and town planning could support maritime commerce.

In 2005, the Mumbai Metropolitan Region Heritage Conservation Society approved assistance for a conservation project proposed by the Naval Dockyard. The plan included protecting the structure and developing interpretation through a centre or museum.

That effort reminds us that smaller buildings can explain a city as clearly as its grand monuments. Ballard Bunder invites us to look for Mumbai's maritime history within its urban fabric.
