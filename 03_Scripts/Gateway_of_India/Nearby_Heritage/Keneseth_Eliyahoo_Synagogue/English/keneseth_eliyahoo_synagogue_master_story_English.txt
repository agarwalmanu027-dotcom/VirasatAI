Keneseth Eliyahoo Synagogue introduces the Jewish heritage of Mumbai through a building made for worship and community. Constructed in 1884, this Sephardic synagogue brings a different architectural voice to the city's historic centre.

Its classical revival design is enriched by stained glass, teakwood furnishings, decorative plasterwork and iron details. Notice how these elements combine practical structure with careful ornament. Colour, filtered light and craftsmanship give the interior a character that cannot be understood from the exterior alone.

The synagogue also tells a story of conservation. Restoration supported by World Monuments Fund received a UNESCO Asia-Pacific Award of Merit in 2019, recognising work that recovered important historic details while respecting the building's religious purpose.

This is a place of faith as well as an architectural landmark. Understanding that connection helps us appreciate Mumbai's heritage through the communities that sustain it, rather than through buildings alone.
