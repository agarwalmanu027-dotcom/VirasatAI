Gurdwara Mata Kaulan, also known as Kaulsar Sahib, brings a personal story of devotion into Amritsar's wider sacred landscape. It remembers Bibi Kaulan, who is associated with Guru Hargobind.

The adjoining Kaulsar pool is an essential part of the place. Official tourism describes its excavation in the sixteen-twenties. Together, water and memorial space create a setting for remembrance beyond the central shrine of Harmandir Sahib.

Look at how the pool gives the buildings room to be seen. In Amritsar, sacred water is connected with religious practice and with the shape of the city. Kaulsar adds its own identity to that larger pattern.

Follow the gurdwara's customs and approach the place as a living religious site. Mata Kaulan's memorial reminds us that a sacred city preserves individual lives as well as the histories of its most famous institutions.
