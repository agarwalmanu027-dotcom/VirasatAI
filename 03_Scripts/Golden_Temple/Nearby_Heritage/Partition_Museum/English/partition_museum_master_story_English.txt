The Partition Museum in Amritsar asks us to understand history through the lives of people who experienced it. Housed in the historic Town Hall, it preserves memories connected with the Partition of nineteen forty-seven.

Personal objects, archival documents, art and oral histories bring the scale of that event closer. A small possession can carry the memory of a home, a journey or a family separated by violence and displacement.

The museum's approach encourages careful listening. Instead of reducing Partition to a line on a map, it gives attention to the people whose lives changed around that line.

Allow time for individual stories and handle the subject with sensitivity. This is the Amritsar museum; the institution also has a separate site in Delhi. Here, the Town Hall has become a setting for remembrance, reflection and a more human understanding of the past.
