The Zenana Enclosure presents another side of Hampi's architecture. Within a walled royal-area setting, the Lotus Mahal and surviving watchtowers create a different impression from the city's tall temple gateways.

The Lotus Mahal brings together curved arches and layered roof forms. Its appearance encourages comparison with nearby religious buildings, showing the range of designs used in the Vijayanagara landscape.

The familiar name Zenana is traditionally connected with royal women, but it does not settle the original purpose of every structure. Ruins often preserve a plan more clearly than a complete account of how rooms were used.

Look at the walls, entrances and remaining foundations as well as the celebrated pavilion. Beyond the enclosure stand the elephant stables. Together, these places suggest the complexity of the royal centre, where different buildings supported many aspects of court life.
