Amar Sagar Jain Temple combines a sacred building with a wider landscape of water and palace architecture. Official Rajasthan descriptions place a carefully carved Jain temple at one end of the Amar Sagar lake setting, beside the area associated with Amar Singh Palace.

Take in the surroundings before concentrating on a single detail. A temple's overall shape creates one impression, while its carved surfaces invite another, more patient kind of looking. Repeated patterns and changes in light can make the same part of a building appear different as you move.

This is also a place of religious significance. Follow the current instructions for entry and photography, and give worshippers priority over sightseeing. Do not infer the age of every architectural part from its style alone. A quiet visit to Amar Sagar can bring together several ways of appreciating heritage: devotion, skilled stonework and the relationship between a building and the landscape around it.
