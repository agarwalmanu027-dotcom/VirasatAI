Kuldhara is an abandoned village in the Jaisalmer region, associated with the Paliwal Brahmins and protected by the State Archaeology Department. Its surviving settlement invites us to look closely at the shape of a desert community.

Walk through the permitted lanes and consider the outlines of homes. A doorway, a wall or the direction of a street can help you think about how people shared space and organised everyday life. Looking at several homes together reveals more of the village's layout than focusing on a single ruin.

Stories of a sudden departure and a curse are part of the folklore surrounding Kuldhara. These tales add a layer of popular memory to the empty settlement.

Protect the ruins and avoid climbing weak walls. Kuldhara encourages patient observation of the traces left by its former residents. Even when a place is no longer inhabited, its streets and buildings can keep a community's presence visible.
