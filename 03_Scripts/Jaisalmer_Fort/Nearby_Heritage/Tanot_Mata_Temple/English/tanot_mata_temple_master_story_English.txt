Tanot Mata Temple is a place of devotion in Rajasthan's border region. Its identity is closely associated with the goddess worshipped here and with the Border Security Force. Official Rajasthan material describes the involvement of a BSF trust, while government tourism records also identify BSF-linked development at the Tanot complex.

For many visitors, the temple carries memories of conflict as well as religious meaning. Stories about protection during wartime have become part of its devotional reputation. Such accounts should be understood as traditions of faith, rather than used as independently verified explanations of physical events.

A respectful visit focuses on the living shrine and the people who care for it. Observe local instructions about access, photography and behaviour, especially because this is a border-region destination. Do not assume a temple visit includes access to nearby military locations. Tanot invites reflection on how belief, service and public memory can become connected around one sacred place.
