Beni Sagar Dam adds open water to Khajuraho's wider landscape. Identified on the government's city base map and included in official tourism material, it offers a different setting from the temple groups.

A reservoir asks us to look at the relationship between the water and its shore. The dam holds water, while changing levels alter how the edges of that landscape appear.

After studying small carved details at the monuments, the broad view here can change the pace of a heritage journey. It is an opportunity to notice sky, water and the land around them.

Use permitted shore areas and confirm local restrictions before considering any activity. Beni Sagar's role in this collection is a landscape stop, without a promise of boating or swimming. It helps place Khajuraho's celebrated buildings within a wider environment shaped by water as well as architecture.
