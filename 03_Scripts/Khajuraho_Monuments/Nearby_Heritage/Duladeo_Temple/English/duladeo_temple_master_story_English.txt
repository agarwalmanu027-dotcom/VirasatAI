Duladeo Temple brings us to the southern group at Khajuraho and to a later chapter of Chandela temple building. Also known as Kunwar Math, the temple is dedicated to Shiva.

Its hall has an octagonal form, giving the interior its own spatial character. From the broad plan, turn to the smaller carved details: graceful figures, ornaments and celestial forms reveal the care given to the stone surface.

The tower and its subsidiary forms connect Duladeo with the wider Khajuraho architectural tradition, while its individual details make comparison worthwhile. A group of monuments becomes more interesting when we notice their differences as well as their similarities.

Leave the carvings untouched and look patiently at one feature at a time. Duladeo helps us see temple art as a developing tradition, with later craftspeople working within familiar forms while giving a building its own expression.
