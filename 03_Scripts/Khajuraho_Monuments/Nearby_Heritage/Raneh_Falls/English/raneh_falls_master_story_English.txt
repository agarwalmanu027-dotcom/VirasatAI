Raneh Falls introduce a different kind of carving near Khajuraho. Here, the Ken River has shaped a rocky canyon, and waterfalls move through a landscape of coloured stone.

The district's description highlights granite in tones of pink, red and grey. Look at the rock surfaces as carefully as the water. Changes of colour and texture are an important part of the place's character.

Several falls belong to the canyon, and their appearance changes with seasonal flow. A photograph from one month cannot promise exactly the same view in another.

From permitted viewpoints, notice how the water follows the rock rather than a straight, even channel. Keep behind barriers and leave the river habitat undisturbed. Raneh offers a useful contrast to Khajuraho's temples: one landscape was shaped by human skill, while this one records the continuing work of water and rock.
