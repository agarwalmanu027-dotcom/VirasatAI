Astaranga Beach adds the life of Odisha's fishing coast to this heritage journey. The destination is known for colourful evening skies and the setting of a coastal fishing community.

Here, the sea and shore are more than a scenic background. They are part of the surroundings in which people work and organise everyday life. Looking at the wider landscape helps us recognise this connection without treating it as a stage made only for visitors.

Watch the changing light across the coast and notice the meeting of land, water and sky. Even a place without a grand palace or temple can have a strong identity.

Give working people space and ask before photographing them closely. Follow local guidance around the water and leave the sand clean. Astaranga encourages a broader view of heritage, where natural surroundings and the routines of a community are also worth understanding and respecting.
