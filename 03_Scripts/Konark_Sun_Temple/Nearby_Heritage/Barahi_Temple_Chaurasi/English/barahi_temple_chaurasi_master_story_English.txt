Barahi Temple at Chaurasi offers a different shape within Odisha's temple architecture. Also written as Varahi, the shrine is dedicated to the goddess Varahi in the Prachi valley.

Its most distinctive architectural feature is the barrel-vaulted roof. This belongs to the Khakhara form, which gives the temple an elongated outline rather than the soaring profile familiar from many other Odisha shrines.

Begin by looking at the building as a whole. The roof's shape changes how the eye moves across the structure. Then notice how the carved surfaces and openings fit within that overall form.

Respect the sacred setting and follow guidance on entry and photography. Avoid touching carved details. Chaurasi's Barahi Temple shows why regional architecture should not be reduced to one standard design. Different forms answered different religious and artistic needs, leaving a varied heritage that becomes clearer when we look carefully at each individual building.
