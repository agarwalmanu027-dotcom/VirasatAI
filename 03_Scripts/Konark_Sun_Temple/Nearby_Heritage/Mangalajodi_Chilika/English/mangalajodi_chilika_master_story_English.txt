Mangalajodi presents heritage through a living wetland and the people who care for it. In the Chilika region, marshes and channels support resident birds as well as seasonal visitors arriving from farther away.

The place is also known for a community-led change from bird poaching towards protection and nature-based livelihoods. Local knowledge has become part of caring for the habitat and helping visitors understand it.

Traditional boats offer a close view of the wetland landscape. Watch how birds feed, move and share the shallow water, while remembering that the wildlife present changes with season and conditions.

Keep voices low, follow local guides and give birds space. Mangalajodi shows that conservation can be a continuing community practice. Its story connects a natural setting with decisions made by people, reminding us that protecting a place also means supporting the knowledge and care that help it survive.
