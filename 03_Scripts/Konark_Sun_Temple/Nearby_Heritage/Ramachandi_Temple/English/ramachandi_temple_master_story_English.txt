Ramachandi Temple brings faith into a coastal landscape of river, sand and sea. The shrine honours Maa Ramachandi and forms part of the sacred setting along the Puri to Konark route.

Nearby, the Kushabhadra River meets the Bay of Bengal. This meeting of waters helps give the place its character, while the temple gives visitors a focus for worship and local devotion.

Take time to notice the relationship between the shrine and its surroundings. A sacred place can draw its identity from the landscape around it as well as from the building itself.

Respect worshippers and follow the temple's guidance on photography and entry. Keep the riverbank and shore clean, and observe the water from safe, permitted areas. Ramachandi adds a living religious experience to a journey through Odisha's heritage. It reminds us that coastal places can hold both natural beauty and meanings carried by the communities who worship there.
