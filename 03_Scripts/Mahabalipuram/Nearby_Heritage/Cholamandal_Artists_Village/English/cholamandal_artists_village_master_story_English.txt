Cholamandal Artists' Village asks a simple question: how can artists make space for a life devoted to art? Established in nineteen sixty-six at Injambakkam, this community developed places to live, work and share creative ideas. Its significance lies in the collective effort behind the studios and galleries as much as in the finished artworks.

Cholamandal is associated with the Madras Movement of Art, an important part of modern art in South India. Paintings, drawings and sculptures encourage you to look beyond the expectation that heritage must always be ancient. A work can express cultural memory through a new shape, an unusual surface or a personal visual language.

Take time with one artwork before moving to the next. Ask what its material contributes, and how its form changes your first impression. Studios are working spaces, so respect privacy and photography instructions. Cholamandal shows that heritage also grows through people creating together, making room for experiments and passing ideas to another generation.
