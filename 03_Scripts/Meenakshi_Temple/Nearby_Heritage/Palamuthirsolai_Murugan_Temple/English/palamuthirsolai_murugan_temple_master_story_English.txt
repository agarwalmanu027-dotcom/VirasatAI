Palamuthirsolai Murugan Temple, also called Pazhamudircholai, belongs to the hill landscape of Solaimalai near Madurai. It is recognised as one of the six sacred abodes of Murugan, also known as Subramaniya.

The hill setting is important to the experience. This shrine lies above the Alagarkoil temple area, but the two places have different dedications. Keeping their identities clear helps visitors understand the religious landscape.

For devotees, the temple is part of a larger pilgrimage tradition connecting Murugan's six abodes. For a heritage visitor, it shows how sacred geography can extend beyond the city's monumental centre into surrounding hills.

Observe the setting without disturbing worship and follow local instructions. Palamuthirsolai adds another layer to a Madurai journey, bringing religious continuity and the natural environment together in a place shaped by pilgrimage.
