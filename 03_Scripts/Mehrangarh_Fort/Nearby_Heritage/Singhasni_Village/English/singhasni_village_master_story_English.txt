Singhasni Village brings the heritage journey into the world of pottery in the Jodhpur region. Its craft connection invites us to think about the people whose skilled work gives everyday objects their shape and purpose.

Take a moment to consider a clay vessel. Its form, the way it can be held, and the use it is made for all reflect choices. Looking closely at a simple object can reveal the care and effort behind it. Craft also connects cultural knowledge with the practical need to earn a living.

If a workshop visit has been arranged locally, give the artisans time and space to work. Ask before taking photographs, and show respect for their work and belongings. Singhasni adds a human scale to a journey around Jodhpur, encouraging us to recognise pottery as skilled work and a part of the region's cultural life.
