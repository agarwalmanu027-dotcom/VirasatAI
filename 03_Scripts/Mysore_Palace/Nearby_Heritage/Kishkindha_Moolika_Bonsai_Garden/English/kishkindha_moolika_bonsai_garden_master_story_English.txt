Kishkindha Moolika Bonsai Garden brings a different scale to your journey through Mysuru. Within the Sri Ganapathy Sachchidananda Ashram, carefully shaped miniature trees form a living collection.

A bonsai tree asks you to look closely. Its trunk, branches and leaves create the impression of a much larger tree within a small space. The garden's value lies in this patient relationship between natural growth and careful maintenance.

The institution connects parts of the garden with spiritual themes and respect for nature. For visitors, it also offers an opportunity to observe the variety of tree forms and the skill needed to care for them.

Unlike a stone sculpture, a bonsai continues to change. Each plant needs attention as it grows. This garden reminds us that preservation can involve living things as well as buildings, and that beauty often depends on years of quiet, consistent care.
