At the Railway Museum in Mysuru, history is told through machines that once carried people across the country. Locomotives, carriages and railway equipment make this a different kind of heritage stop.

Look closely at the engines. Their wheels, pipes and mechanical parts help explain the physical work behind rail travel. Signalling displays show another side of the story: every journey depended on systems designed to guide trains safely.

The royal coaches bring Mysuru's palace history onto the tracks. The Sri Ranga Pavilion and the Maharani's saloon illustrate how members of the royal household travelled, with arrangements very different from an ordinary passenger carriage.

Together, these exhibits connect engineering with everyday movement and royal life. The museum encourages us to see a train as more than transport. It is also a record of changing technology, skilled labour and the many journeys that helped link places and people.
