Adham Khan's Tomb stands in Mehrauli as a distinctive sixteenth-century memorial. It is associated with Adham Khan, a general under Emperor Akbar, and his mother, Maham Anga.

Its octagonal plan makes it different from a simple square tomb. A dome covers the central chamber, and arched verandahs surround it. Walk around the exterior, where permitted, and notice how the same design changes as you approach from another side.

The building offers a lesson in form and movement. An eight-sided plan gives a memorial several faces while keeping the centre important. Rather than rushing through the surrounding lanes, pause to observe the proportions and the deep openings. This monument adds an early Mughal chapter to Mehrauli, connecting the neighbourhood's many older and later historical layers.
