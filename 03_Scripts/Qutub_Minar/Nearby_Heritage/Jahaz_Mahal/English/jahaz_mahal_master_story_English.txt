Jahaz Mahal stands beside Hauz-i-Shamsi in Mehrauli. Its name means Ship Palace, a popular description linked to the way the building's reflection could appear in the surrounding water.

The structure is associated with the Lodi period. Its pavilions and openings make it an interesting companion to the reservoir. Notice the chhatris, the small roofed pavilions that give the upper outline a distinctive character.

The original use of the building has been interpreted in different ways, so it is better to let the surviving architecture guide the visit. Look at the relationship between stone, open space and water. Jahaz Mahal adds an unusual silhouette to Mehrauli's heritage and reminds us that a monument's setting can become part of its name and the way people remember it.
