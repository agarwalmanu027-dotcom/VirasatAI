Naughara brings residential heritage into the market surroundings of Kinari Bazaar. The lane is associated with historic havelis, documented in Delhi's heritage studies as the Naughara Mansions.

Here, the story is not centred on a single royal monument. It is about a group of homes and their relationship with a neighbourhood. Doorways, façades and the shared street can all help us understand that relationship.

Observe from public space and remember that a heritage building can also be someone's home. Respecting privacy is part of appreciating a living neighbourhood. Naughara encourages a slower look at Old Delhi's smaller architectural spaces. It shows why conservation should include residential lanes as well as forts and temples: the everyday fabric of a city carries cultural memory too.
