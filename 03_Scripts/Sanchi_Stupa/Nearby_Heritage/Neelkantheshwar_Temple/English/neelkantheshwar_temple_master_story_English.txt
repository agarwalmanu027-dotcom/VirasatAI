The Neelkantheshwar Temple in Udaipur village belongs to Madhya Pradesh's Vidisha district. Also known as Udayeshwar, it should not be confused with places in the Rajasthan city of Udaipur.

This Shiva temple is associated with the Paramara ruler Udayaditya. Its inscriptions place construction between ten fifty-nine and ten eighty. Such records are valuable because they connect the surviving architecture with a documented period.

The shrine, hall and entrance porches give the building a sequence of spaces. Notice how movement through those spaces changes your view of the temple. The stonework is both a religious setting and evidence of medieval design.

A date carved into a monument can seem small beside its architecture, but it helps the whole story become clearer. Here, writing and building work together. This temple adds a carefully documented medieval chapter to the wider heritage landscape associated with Sanchi.
