Sonari reminds us that Sanchi's Buddhist history extends beyond its best-known hill. Here, surviving stupas form another part of the same regional heritage landscape.

A stupa is a sacred structure, and its rounded form is designed to be approached from the outside. At a smaller archaeological site, attention can move from the overall shape to the way stones were arranged. What survives may be incomplete, but even remains can reveal the care given to a sacred place.

Sonari is officially protected, including the land beside its stupas. That setting matters. Heritage is held not only in a structure, but also in the space that allows us to understand it.

After seeing the elaborate gateways at Sanchi, Sonari offers another way of looking. Follow the outline of a stupa, notice the material, and consider how several communities could contribute to a connected Buddhist landscape.
