A monument's surroundings can change the way we see it. Taj Nature Walk, near the eastern gate of the Taj Mahal, brings the famous building into a setting of greenery and open views.

This is a landscape and nature attraction, rather than another royal tomb. Paths and viewing points encourage you to notice the space around the monument. The Taj appears as part of the horizon, with trees and the changing sky shaping the view.

A slower walk can make this a useful pause during a heritage visit. Look at the contrast between the carefully shaped monument and the living landscape around it. The experience also reminds us that protecting heritage means caring for its surroundings. Keeping paths and green areas clean helps preserve the setting in which a monument is appreciated.
