Jorasanko Thakur Bari brings the life of Rabindranath Tagore into the setting of a family home. Tagore was born here in 1861 and spent important parts of his life in this ancestral house. The site now houses the Rabindra Bharati Museum.

The significance of Jorasanko extends beyond one celebrated writer. The Tagore family helped shape literature, art and cultural debate in Bengal. Artists Gaganendranath and Abanindranath Tagore also belong to this wider household story.

A house museum offers a particular kind of encounter with history. Rooms and family surroundings help connect creative work with the environment in which people lived. The building becomes a setting for understanding ideas, relationships and memory.

Look at Jorasanko as both a home and a cultural record. It introduces a world in which artistic experimentation and intellectual life became part of the heritage of Kolkata.
