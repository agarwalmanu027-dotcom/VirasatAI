Magen David Synagogue is a landmark of Kolkata's Jewish heritage. Opened in 1884 and associated with the Ezra family's patronage, it brings a distinctive religious and architectural presence to the Burrabazar area.

The brick-red exterior and tall tower create a strong outline. Inside, a central nave and side aisles organise the prayer space. The tebah, a raised platform used for readings and prayer, is an important part of that arrangement. Decorative surfaces and stained glass add to the interior's character.

A synagogue is understood through the community and traditions it serves. Its architecture provides a setting for shared worship, while its history records the place of Jewish residents within the city.

Take time to connect the visible features with that purpose. Magen David helps tell a fuller story of Kolkata, in which religious diversity and community patronage shaped the urban landscape.
