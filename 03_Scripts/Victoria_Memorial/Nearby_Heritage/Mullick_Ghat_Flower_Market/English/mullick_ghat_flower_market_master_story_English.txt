Mullick Ghat Flower Market shows heritage through work rather than through a single monumental building. On the Kolkata side of the Hooghly, near Howrah Bridge, the market connects flowers, traders, growers and the demands of the city.

The colours are an immediate attraction, but the activity behind them is just as important. Flowers must be gathered, transported, arranged and exchanged. A garland or bundle represents the efforts of several people before it reaches a customer.

Research on the market documents these relationships and the spaces in which selling takes place. That perspective helps us see the market as part of Kolkata's social and economic life.

Observe without getting in the way of work. Mullick Ghat invites us to consider a broader meaning of heritage, where skills, livelihoods and everyday exchange can be as significant as stone and marble.
